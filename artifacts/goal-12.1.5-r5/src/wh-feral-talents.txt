BEST FERAL DRUID MYTHIC+ TALENT BUILD
Much the same as raid builds the two hero talents are incredibly competititive, you can really play either of the builds below. The key talents in this build are self explanatory,  원시 분노,  최상위 포식자의 갈망, and  날뛰는 야성, with Midnight we also have new toys to play around with such as  날뛰는 광란 which converts one of our short CDs into a burst AOE tool.
Wildstalker
Druid of the Claw
M+ builds are fairly flexible, while there are some mandatory talents mentioned above such as  원시 분노,  최상위 포식자의 갈망, and  날뛰는 야성, there are also a lot of swaps you can make in other locations to maximise your single target if you feel its needed for a particular dungeon.

BEST FERAL DRUID DELVE TALENT BUILD
These talents are pretty much identical to the M+ builds. Delves require largely the same tools that M+ does. You may have a bit more leeway to maximise single target with talents like  집중된 광란 but generally this is unneeded.
Druid of the Claw
Delve builds are fairly flexible, while there are some mandatory talents mentioned above such as  원시 분노,  최상위 포식자의 갈망, and  날뛰는 야성, there are also a lot of swaps you can make in other locations to maximise your single target if you feel its needed for a particular delve.
