Talent Disclaimer


The default talents assume the new 4-Set Tier bonus. Put  머무는 그림자 into  짙은 어둠 if you haven't unlocked the bonus.
Subtlety maintains its advantage of universally strong talent options, which leads to a very limited set of talent changes between content types.  Deathstalker points with slightly higher pure single-target damage and stronger priority damage, while  Trickster offers a simpler, more forgiving gameplay and slightly better AoE. Both hero talents are close in output, and my suggestion would be to start with  Trickster if you are a new player and try  Deathstalker if you want to optimize your single-target output further.  Trickster provides stronger burst and AoE too, which makes it an excellent overall pick.

BEST SUBTLETY ROGUE DELVE TALENT BUILD
Delves offer short fights and a lot of opportunities for restealths between enemies. This talent build focuses on burst damage and control.  공수 자극제 is taken for control and, as a last resort, gives the opportunity to use  실명 as a ranged interrupt/control spell frequently. Applying  속임수 거래 to your delve companion is still a potent strategy for survival.  Trickster is easier to play, and as such is suggested as the default choice.
Trickster
Deathstalker
There are only a few adjustments you can make to delve content. The list below highlights some potential talent swaps.
If you need more AoE:  짙은 어둠 →  강력한 가루.
If you need an enrage dispell:  공수 자극제 →  독칼.
