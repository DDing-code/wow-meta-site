BEST BLOOD DEATH KNIGHT MYTHIC+ TALENT BUILD
The Mythic+ builds we provide make trade-offs for AoE and general resourcefulness in a content type that frequently enforces downtime and short burts of combat.
Deathbringer
San'layn
Unlike  San'layn,  Deathbringer provides a mostly plug-and-play experience in Mythic+. It does so at the cost of a significant amount of single-target and AoE damage, but it in turn also brings defensive perks that are difficult to ignore: an entire 7.5% damage reduction, significantly more healing from  피의 역병, and a completely pain-free  뼈의 보호막 experience.

We have provided two builds, with one of them featuring  흡혈의 구 for ease of use. Unlike previous expansions, this is a relatively cost-free choice.

Playing  San'layn is a miserable experience in delves due to the number of non-combat scenarios spent collecting things or walking from place to place. For this reason - and many others - we only recommend picking  Deathbringer for this type of content.
Deathbringer
This is the  Deathbringer Mythic+ build with all group elements removed from it. You're mostly going solo into Delves, so why would you want to waste a talent point on a Runic Power reduction on Raise Dead when Valeera cannot be combat ressed?
