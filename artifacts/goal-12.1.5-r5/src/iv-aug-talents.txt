Best Midnight Talents for Augmentation Evoker

Augmentation has two Hero Talents that focus on different aspects of the class.  Chronowarden has a greater focus on  Prescience and the power of your buffs. Meanwhile,  Scalecommander focuses on higher damage output with buffs to  Eruption and additional damage sources like  Bombardments.

Additionally,  Chronowarden will feel more fast paced as it grants additional  Essence Burst procs and Haste buffs which accelerate your rotation.

We are recommending  Chronowarden for the default raiding choice, as it will vastly outperform  Scalecommander in all scenarios in Raid. We currently recommend playing  Scalecommander in weekly keys and  Chronowarden for higher keys.

 Chronowarden Raid Build

This build focuses on increasing the power of your buffs and deals 15% more DPS than  Scalecommander.  Chronowarden synergises extremely well with our Season 2 Tier Set due to having dedicated nodes for  Upheaval and more support for  Prescience.

We play  Time Skip in this build in order to free up an additional point to take  Overlord which adds significant damage when you can cleave multiple targets with  Breath of Eons. Furthermore, currently  Time Skip with either  Overlord or  Inferno's Blessing performs approximately 0.5% better than  Interwoven Threads baseline.

One of the biggest considerations on whether to play  Interwoven Threads,  Tomorrow, Today or base  Time Skip comes down to  Breath of Eons timings. Depending on the fight you can find that  Breath of Eons lines up naturally better with adds or other important moments better with either talent. This improvement is enough to outperform the slight default bias towards  Time Skip so we recommend trying out both options and playing what feels best.

When playing the  Time Skip variant you will want to use a cancelaura macro for  Tip the Scales. For more information see our rotation guide.

Chronowarden Cancelaura Tip the Scales

Additionally, if simply do not enjoy playing  Time Skip you can move the point from  Overlord to  Interwoven Threads, for a loss of approximately 0.5% less DPS, but gaining significantly smoother gameplay and slightly different cooldown timings.

Our Raid build starts out with a universal talent setup that will work everywhere but not every boss will need you to have  Quell. If you are on a boss that has no interrupts this we suggest moving them to other utility like  Arcane Reach,  Bestow Weyrnstone or  Aspects' Favor. This is a very minor benefit so we opted to include it always by default!

Mythic+
Omnium Folio

The Omnium Folio was a new system added in Midnight's Patch 12.0.7. After a short questline, you will be able to choose from several small bonuses that provide additional throughput, survivability or utility. We recommend completing these quests as soon as possible, as this system will be active for the rest of Midnight and provides a noticeable boost in power.

We currently suggest the following talents as your default progression path. As you can swap the runes at any time, you can always adjust these later based on the encounter or personal preference once you are more comfortable:

First Rune:  Rune of Unleashed Fire
Second Rune:  Rune of Self-Mending
Third Rune:  Rune of Lingering
Fourth Rune:  Rune of Masterful Cunning
Fifth Rune:  Rune of Overload

Apex Talent:  Duplicate

In Midnight Augmentation Evoker gains access to the Apex Talent:  Duplicate. This Apex Talent and its upgrades have significant impact on Augmentation Evoker's Talent Choices and Stat Priority.

ADVERTISEMENT

The First Point  Duplicate summons a Pet after you cast  Breath of Eons that deals significant damage while active. This Pet casts  Eruption,  Fire Breath, and  Upheaval. These casts will not extend  Ebon Might. The  Eruption casts will scale with all of Augmentation's talents except for  Mass Eruption and  Melt Armor.

The Second and Third Apex Talent Points  Duplicate allows anything that triggers  Sands of Time to also extend  Duplicate. This leads to a minigame very similar to maintaining  Ebon Might where you attempt to maximise the uptime of  Duplicate. This considerably increases the power of Haste and Critical Strike as these boost the uptime of  Duplicate. Additionally, it significantly boosts the power of the  Breath of Eons' talents  Imminent Destruction and  Plot the Future as they increase the amount of casts you can get within your  Duplicate.

The Final Apex Talent point,  Duplicate, makes the Duplicate provide an incredibly powerful buff effect that increases your  Ebon Might on allies by 75% and significantly boosts the damage of your  Upheaval.

ADVERTISEMENT

Another significance of  Duplicate is that it heavily incentivises pressing  Breath of Eons on cooldown. Maximising uptime on the doubled Primary Stat from the Buff ultimately contributes more for overall DPS than holding  Breath of Eons for an ideal damage window.

Even though  Breath of Eons' amplifier is a more significant DPS boost than the 12% Primary Stat increase from having your  Duplicate up with  Duplicate, using  Breath of Eons on cooldown results in significantly higher  Duplicate uptime due to more frequent casts that get extended by  Duplicate.

At the same time, you will still get a sufficiently strong  Breath of Eons window due to how varied most DPS are in their damage profile.

ADVERTISEMENT
Hero Talents

Hero Talents become available at Level 71 and are mutually exclusive trees that offer slightly different gameplay and visuals. As an Augmentation Evoker you can pick between  Scalecommander and  Chronowarden. We go into some of the details and differences between them below!

 Scalecommander

 Scalecommander is the Personal Damage focused Hero Talent Tree that has the least emphasis on Buff based gameplay instead opting to boost Empower Spells and Essence Spenders. This is perfect for anyone who wants fire and forget gameplay and does not want to track their co-DPS's cooldowns.

Currently we recommend  Scalecommander in Mythic+ for weekly keys as it functions better with worse performing allies. In optimal conditions  Chronowarden can significantly outperform  Scalecommander, but both are viable at all levels of Mythic+, depending on what you enjoy the most.

ADVERTISEMENT

For Raiding  Scalecommander is 15% behind  Chronowarden and we strongly recommend against playing it.

The core focus of  Scalecommander gameplay comes from applying  Bombardments with  Eruption after gaining stacks from using  Fire Breath and  Upheaval. After applying  Bombardments you want to extend it as much as possible by casting  Eruption while having the talent  Extended Battle. These bombardments add additional damage procs from your allies which ends up doing a considerable amount of damage over time!

Another central pillar of  Scalecommander gameplay is  Mass Eruption. After casting each of your empower spells you will gain a stack of  Mass Eruption which will allow your next  Eruption cast to cleave dealing additional hits to other nearby enemies. This is especially important now that  Wingleader is tied to  Mass Eruption instead of  Bombardments. The extra cooldown reduction provided to  Breath of Eons provides makes  Scalecommander lean into  Breath of Eons talents like  Imminent Destruction and  Plot the Future. All combined, you are strongly incentivised to maximise the overall casts of  Breath of Eons for personal benefit instead of opting for higher quality casts that deal more damage from allies.

ADVERTISEMENT
 Chronowarden

 Chronowarden is the Buff Oriented Hero Talent Tree that is themed around Time Magic. As a  Chronowarden a larger fraction of your damage comes from Buffs than  Scalecommander since you get stronger  Prescience and  Ebon Might buffs from  Nozdormu Adept and  Double-time. This tree is perfect for players who want their friends to be the strongest they can be and, is slightly more forgiving for personal play.

 Chronowarden enhances your  Living Flame turning it into  Chrono Flame augmenting it with bronze magic that deals additional damage based off of damage dealt recently.  Chrono Flame still functions as a normal  Living Flame that interacts fully with all  Living Flame talents, with each projectile fired dealing additional  Chrono Flame damage.

This is currently our recommended Hero Talent tree for High End Mythic+ and Raiding as it outperforms  Scalecommander considerably.

ADVERTISEMENT

If you are playing  Time Skip as a  Chronowarden you will want to use a Cancelaura Macro for  Tip the Scales. This lets you keep the cooldowns of  Time Skip and  Tip the Scales lined up. You need to do this because the cooldown of  Tip the Scales only starts when the buff is consumed.

Cancelaura Tip the Scales Macro

 Chronowarden does not add any additional rotational gameplay elements that you have to play around. Instead, you just get you more resources and stats. You gain more Haste from  Primacy and  Temporal Burst, and better resource generation from  Chronal Dynamo and  Afterimage, to help the performance your base rotation. These resources and stats makes it significantly easier to maintain your buffs.

PvP Talents (War Mode)

Enabling War Mode grants you several benefits in the open world which you made decide are beneficial. We have listed the main benefits below.

ADVERTISEMENT
PvP talents enabled in the outdoor world.
At least 10% increase in World Quest rewards at maximum level.
10% more experience gained while leveling.
Earn Conquest Points, which can reward gear every week.

We do not recommend enabling War Mode for general leveling and PvE content as it allows other players to kill you which is incredibly frustrating. The War Mode talents available to Augmentation Evoker are not a considerable time save as none of them provide a significant increase of DPS. That said, it is optimal to enable it if you are willing to engage in PvP.

Augmentation Evoker PvP talents barely provide any noticeable speed up in combat. We have listed the best ones to have active below.

 Nullifying Shroud is recommended as it gives you an easy way to immune loss of control effects from both NPCs and enemy players trying to gank you.
 Obsidian Mettle will allow you to safely cast spells while enemies are trying to knock you back.
 Unburdened Flight can be useful when you are trying to run away from a pack of enemies and not be slowed by Daze.
