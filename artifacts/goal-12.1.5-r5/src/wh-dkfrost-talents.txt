BEST FROST DEATH KNIGHT MYTHIC+ TALENT BUILD
Frost builds the same in Mythic+ as it does in raid.  옥죄는 공세 carries AoE damage, and  냉기파멸 does not hit hard enough anymore to be a good option. This leaves Frost with very little build variety in Mythic+.
DEATHBRINGER
This build is restrictive on swaps.  서리수확자 is the easiest swap since it is not required for pathing, but boss damage is always valuable in Mythic+, so we keep it most of the time.
