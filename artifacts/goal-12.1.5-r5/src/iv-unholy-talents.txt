Best Midnight Talents for Unholy Death Knight

Welcome to the talent guide for Unholy Death Knight. Here we will discuss all matters associated with your talent choices, providing the best available builds as well as details regarding alternative picks and potential talent swaps.

Unholy Death Knight can choose from two slightly different styles when it comes to talents; plagues or minions. Minion builds synergise well with  Rider of the Apocalypse and can also be paired with  San'layn for our recommended Mythic+ build. These builds pick talents such as  Lord of the Dead and  Outnumber. Plague builds generally run  San'layn with the  Blightfall talent to really harness the DoT power.

For single-target raid encounters, we recommend the  Rider of the Apocalypse minion build. For Mythic+, we recommend the  San'layn pet build.

Raid Talents

This  San'layn minion build is an alternative for raids. It focuses heavily on minion damage with both  Lord of the Dead,  Grave Mastery, as well as the minions we summon through these talents. It is easy to play and deals a good amount of burst damage.

To gain some better AoE burst with the build, pick up  Menacing Magus instead of  Infected Claws. This loses a few percent of damage in single-target, but adds a significant amount of cleave. If you want even more cleave, pick up  Cycle of Death as well instead of  Ghoulish Frenzy. You are then running the full San'layn Pet Mythic+ build.

Mythic+
Omnium Folio

The Omnium Folio is a new system introduced in Midnight's Patch 12.0.7, and offers some minor performance-enhancing customization options after a multi-week quest series. Some of these bonuses affect your damage output, while others are more defensive in nature.

ADVERTISEMENT

We recommend you pick the following bonuses in the first couple of weeks, but as you can freely switch between them at any time, feel free to experiment with other options yourself if you feel you do not get enough mileage out of the recommended options:

Week 1: Rune of Unleashed Fire
Week 2: Rune of Void-Tainted Shell
Week 3: Rune of Lingering
Week 4: Rune of Critical Power
Week 5: Rune of Overload
Apex Talent:  Forbidden Knowledge

Unholy Death Knight gets access to  Forbidden Knowledge Apex Talents as you level up from 80 to 90 in Midnight. The first rank provides a 30 seconds buff once you cast  Army of the Dead, transforming your  Death Coil into  Necrotic Coil and your  Epidemic into  Graveyard. These new abilities are powerful version of the baseline ones, notably adding an AoE component to  Death Coil. The impact to the rotation is that  Necrotic Coil is used at up to 5 targets in AoE ( Death Coil is only used at up to 3).

Rank 2 and 3 ( Forbidden Knowledge) gives Mastery when  Putrefy is cast and adds a stack of  Lesser Ghoul. Slightly less  Festering Strikes are needed in the rotation but not much else.

ADVERTISEMENT

Rank 4 ( Forbidden Knowledge) gives  Dread Plague a 20% chance to trigger  Putrefy at 100% effectiveness, significantly increasing the number of  Putrefy triggered in the rotation overall.

It is crucial to maintain good  Dread Plague and  Festering Scythe uptime to get the most of this talent. Furthermore, your pets must be on assist for it to function properly.

Hero Talents

Available at level 71, Hero talents are mutually exclusive trees you can choose to slightly alter gameplay. Unholy Death Knight can choose between  Rider of the Apocalypse and  San'layn.

ADVERTISEMENT
 Rider of the Apocalypse

The  Rider of the Apocalypse Hero Talents focus on minion damage, summoning Horsemen to deal heavy damage to your enemies. You have minor rotational updates with Trollbane's Chains of Ice, where you want to burst them ASAP and you want to stay in Mograine's  Death and Decay to get 5% damage boost. Otherwise, the main benefit comes from  Death Charge which is an amazing mobility ability for Death Knights!

 San'layn

The  San'layn Hero Talents focus more on Plague damage and direct damage via  Vampiric Strike and  Death Coil and  Epidemic boosts. Furthermore, it has an interesting quadratic scaling component via the  Blood Beast ability and can run  Blightfall which is a fun an interesting talent to play around. The main rotational difference is the introduction of  Vampiric Strike that we get to press lots in our  Dark Transformation window. We also try and proc it in-between our Dark Transformation windows to maintain the  Essence of the Blood Queen buff.

PvP Talents (War Mode)

PvP Talents are specific talents usable in PvP content. We can leverage these in open-world PvP to get some nice buffs.

PvP talents enabled in the outdoor world.
15% more experience and quest awards gained while leveling.

With the benefits of enabling War Mode for leveling and PvE content, it is recommended enabling the feature to maximize your leveling and rewards at maximum level. However, you will make yourself available for open-world PvP, and the possibility of being "ganked" while leveling or doing World Quests exists.

Below, we will rank the PvP talents best used for leveling and doing solo or small group PvE content.

ADVERTISEMENT
 Life and Death heals you for 10% of the amount your target afflicted by  Dread Plague receives. Not relevant for PvE really.
 Dark Simulacrum places a debuff on your enemy, activating when they cast a spell that uses mana. It allows you to use that same spell for some time. Not useful in PvE content.
 Necrotic Wounds - your  Putrefy now applies a Necrotic Wound to the target, absorbing 8% of all healing received for the next 15 seconds (max 3 stacks). Not useful in PvE content.
 Zombify - Costs 30 Runic Power and reanimates a nearby corpse into a zombie. The zombie will slowly walk towards you target, exploding and stunning all enemies in an area if it reaches the target. Somewhat useful stun in PvE content.
 Stitchmaster heals you for 5% of your  Lesser Ghouls max health when they die (about 2.5% of your health).  Death Strike sacrifices all active ghouls when pressed. A nice additional sustain in AoE with a DPS drawback as you might lose lesser ghoul uptime.
 Strangulate silences your target for 4 seconds. Inferior to just running  Asphyxiate (it replaces the talent) and a pretty useless PvE talent.
 Doomburst causes your  Death Coils to deal 25% of your plague damage an additional time and slow the target by 45% for 3 seconds. Useful PvE talent is it is a DPS increase.
 Rot and Wither adds an absorption shield to any target hit by  Death and Decay for 100% of the damage amount. Useless pretty much.
 Bloodforged Armor provides 4 seconds of 25% physical damage reduction whenever you press  Death Strike. Solid defensive gain!
 Spellwarden lowers the cooldown of  Anti-Magic Shell by 10 seconds and allows you to use it on allies. It's just a nice defensive gain because it allows more AMS usage.

 Doomburst is a solid choice due to its nice DPS gain. No other talent does much for us in PvE, and are primarily PvP choices. For a second and third option, we don't have much to choose from.  Bloodforged Armor is generally best due to providing a good defensive gain, and  Spellwarden is a small defensive gain!
