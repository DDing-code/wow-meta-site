Best Midnight Talents for Demonology Warlock

For Season 2, Demonology talent selections remain mostly unchanged, with  Soul Harvester becoming the slightly better choice in pure single-target. Whilst there are still some opportunities for personal preferences and situational adjustments, below are our current recommendations:

Single-Target—  Soul Harvester is tuned around 3% higher than  Diabolist in pure single-target, but suffers from a substantial lack of cleave in comparison.
AoE—  Diabolist is the better choice at any key level. The Mythic+ builds offers comparable single-target to  Soul Harvester, whilst retaining superior AoE.

Omnium Folio

Introduced in Patch 12.0.7, Omnium Folio is a new source of player power that unlocks over the course of few weeks and offers a choice of small but flexible bonuses that can be swapped at any time to match the desired content. The selection of Runes can be tailored to offer throughput, or pivot to survivability and utility.

The following list is the recommended guideline, point by point:

 Rune of Unleashed Fire
 Rune of Self-Mending or  Rune of Void-Tainted Shell
 Rune of Lingering
 Rune of Critical Power
 Rune of Residual Energy and  Rune of Overload offer similar output

Apex Talent:  Dominion of Argus

Available during the leveling in Midnight, these talents are separated from the rest of the spec tree and require subsequent points investment in order to unlock the later ranks.

These talents are strong enough to warrant building the rotation around them, with a shift from a Tyrant setup to a post Tyrant one, aimed at maximising the amount of  Hand of Gul'dan casts and thus additional summons.

Hero Talents

Available at level 71 Hero talents are mutually exclusive trees you can choose to slightly alter gameplay and visuals. Demonology Warlocks can choose between  Diabolist and  Soul Harvester.

 Diabolist

 Diabolist is themed around Demons and the cycle of Overlord, Mother of Chaos and Pit Lord summoned with  Diabolic Ritual. The gameplay as a result is of predictable cadence with a higher degree of burst potential, tied with the Hero Tree summons and spell empowerments.

ADVERTISEMENT

In terms of utility  Soul-Etched Circles gives snare and root immunity on  Demonic Circle: Teleport on top of 50% movement speed but on the other hand  Annihilan's Bellow coupled with  Oppressive Darkness enables 25s cooldown  Howl of Terror that can affect up to 10 targets.

 Soul Harvester

 Soul Harvester identity lies in spending shards very aggressively, in order to benefit from the various DoT effects triggered, and generating more Demonic Core procs through  Sataiel's Volition.

As for utility  Gorebound Fortitude guarantees stronger Healthstones without the need of Soulburn shards,  Friends In Dark Places increase the active mitigation, and  Gorefiend's Resolve brings  Soulstone on par with alternative combat resurrection spells.

PvP Talents (War Mode)

Under our Talents section, there is the option to enter "War Mode." Enabling War Mode provides the following benefits:

PvP talents enabled in the outdoor world
10% increase in World Quest rewards at maximum level
10% more experience gained while leveling
Earn Conquest Points, which can reward gear every week

With the benefits of enabling War Mode for leveling and PvE content, it is recommended you enable the feature. In most situations, this will allow for the maximum returns despite the risks. You will make yourself available for open-world PvP and the possibility to be "ganked" while leveling or doing World Quests exists.

Best War Mode Talents

In this section, we will rank the PvP talents best for leveling and doing solo / small group PvE content. Below is a ranking of both General PvP Talents and Demonology Warlock-specific PvE talents.

 Bonds of Fel allows to burst targets by dragging them away from the marked location.
 Gateway Mastery increases your gateway's range by 20 yards and reduces the duration of the debuff placed on players using it by 15 seconds, making it a good choice when increased mobility is needed.
 Nether Ward summons a shield lasting 3 seconds and reflecting back all harmful spells targeted at you.
 Call Observer is only relevant when dealing with a lot of casting mobs.
 Call Fel Lord guards a location for 15 seconds; any enemies that come within range take up to 5% of maximum Health as damage. This can be used very strategically in certain situations and is capable of dealing some nice burst damage.

 Master Summoner makes  Call Dreadstalkers an instant cast. Since we are often using  Demonic Calling, the quality of life of this talent is diminished. It can be used if you prefer the consistency, but there are generally better choices.
