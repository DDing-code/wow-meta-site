Best Midnight Talents for Protection Warrior

For Season 2, Protection Warrior is in a great position. Both our Hero Talents are viable and can be played everywhere, without hindering the overall performance by too much.

Pure Single-Target -  Colossus is currently leading in single-target damage output.  Mountain Thane is not too far behind, if you fancy that playstyle.
AoE -  Mountain Thane takes the win in AoE damage output. The damage profile it provides is excellent for AoE scenarios like Mythic+. That said, playing  Colossus in keys will not hinder you in any significant way.

Mountain Thane Raid Talents

This build is a decent combination of offensive and defensive talents, making it a good all-around raid build.

 Deep Wounds,  Enduring Alacrity and  Thunderlord can be treated as flex points and placed elsewhere for a more defensive approach.  Tough as Nails is worth the point on bosses which auto-attack you a lot, otherwise, do not take it.

 Heavy Handed can be used on fights where you can consistently cleave on 2-3 targets with  Execute.

Omnium Folio

The Omnium Folio is a progression system introduced in Patch 12.0.7, offering a range of minor customization options that can enhance your character. Unlocked through a timegated questline, the Folio provides a selection of runes that grant offensive, defensive, and utility-focused bonuses.

Below is a list of recommended bonuses to begin with. You can play around with other options as well, as you can switch between them freely.

Week 1: Rune of Unleashed Fire
Week 2: Rune of Self-Mending
Week 3: Rune of Lingering
Week 4: Rune of Critical Power
Week 5: Rune of Overload

 Rune of Burning Haste and  Rune of Critical Power are interchangable with eachother.

ADVERTISEMENT
Apex Talent:  Phalanx

 Phalanx enhances our core rotational abilities,  Thunder Clap and  Shield Slam, increasing their damage done. It also causes your next  Shield Slam that is cast after  Thunder Clap to deal additional damage in a frontal cone.

While the Apex talents enhance our core rotational abilities, they do not provide any meaningful gameplay changes. Just by playing the rotation correctly, you will gain the full benefit of the Apex talents, leading to a overall damage increase.

One thing to note is that our Apex talent are pretty much fully offensive oriented. The  Phalanx does reduce the enemies damage dealt to you by a small amount, but outside of this, its all offensive.

ADVERTISEMENT
Hero Talents

Protection Warrior, upon reaching Level 71, will gain access to one of two extra talent trees called Hero Talents. These two Hero Talents are  Mountain Thane and  Colossus, both offering different playstyles and strengths depending on which you pick.

 Mountain Thane

 Mountain Thane Hero Talents focus on amplifying your already existing abilities. The core of the tree is turning  Thunder Clap into a much more powerful  Thunder Blast. The design of the talents themselves gives us a fun feedback loop that is described below.

 Burst of Power allows  Lightning Strikes to proc  Shield Slam
 Strategist can proc  Shield Slam
 Shield Slam can proc  Thunder Blast
 Thunder Blast can proc  Lightning Strikes through  Flashing Skies

As shown in the list above, the whole tree creates a positive Rage feedback loop while maintaining our rotational fluidness. The better Rage economy is what mainly sets  Mountain Thane and  Colossus apart.

ADVERTISEMENT
 Colossus

 Colossus tree revolves around  Demolish and amplifying the damage of your bleeds,  Revenge and  Shield Slam. While Demolish does not provide any Rage generation, it hits like a truck and enhances our other abilities. It has a different, more methodical flow than the spam heavy  Mountain Thane. You need to put slightly more thought into where to spend the Rage you generate while also trying to maximize each and every cast of  Demolish.

PvP Talents (War Mode)

Opting into War Mode makes you eligible for open-world PvP but also grants you the following benefits:

World Quests at maximum level have 10% increased rewards.
You gain 10% increased experience while leveling.
Grants you the use of PvP talents in the open-world.

With the benefits of enabling War Mode for leveling and PvE content, it is recommended that you enable the feature to maximize your leveling and rewards at maximum level. However, you will make yourself eligible for open-world PvP, and the possibility of being "ganked" while leveling or doing World Quests exists.

ADVERTISEMENT

Below are the recommended PvP talents for leveling and PvE content. You are able to choose one general PvP talent and three spec-specific talents.

 Morale Killer reduces the cooldown of  Demoralizing Shout by 15 seconds, bringing its cooldown to 30 seconds, and causes enemies to deal reduced damage to all targets, not just you. This allows for extremely high uptime on Demoralizing Shout.
 Dragon Charge causes you to charge a long distance at high speed in the targeted direction. It does not require a target. It deals damage and knocks back any enemies that are hit by the charge. The movement that this talent provides is extremely useful for questing or getting out of dangerous situations. It is almost like having another  Heroic Leap in terms of movement.
 Bodyguard is a great tool to help you reduce the Physical damage dealt to your allies.
 Disarm is another extremely strong option you can take. This allows you to significantly reduce the damage output of classes that rely on weapon damage.

These are simply recommendations. You may find other PvP talents more useful depending on your playstyle.
