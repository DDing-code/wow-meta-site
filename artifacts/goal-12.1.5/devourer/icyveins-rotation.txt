 On this page, you will learn how to optimize the rotation of your
Devourer Demon Hunter in both single-target and multi-target
situations. We also have advanced sections about cooldowns, procs, etc.
in order to minmax your DPS. All our content is updated for World of
Warcraft Patch 12.1.5.

Welcome to the Rotation section of our Devourer Demon Hunter guide in
Midnight! Here, we go over all of the gameplay details for World of Warcraft's
newest specialization, covering both single-target and AoE combat styles.
Due to Devourer being a new spec - including new Hero Talent trees - we've covered
how each of them works on our Talents page. If
you're looking to jump straight in, below are some quick-start details on how they

 Annihilator –
 Oriented more towards a pure caster playstyle, it builds up stacks of Voidfall,

Season 2 of Midnight introduces the Abyssal Doomhound's Pursuit, available
from different endgame content. This provides the following bonuses:
 Demon Hunter Devourer 12.1 Class Set 2pc — Harvesting 4 or more Soul
 Fragments with Reap has a 20% chance to cause your next Consume
 to be instant cast and explode in a Soulburst, dealing

 generates 2 Soul Fragments when triggered, and grants Moment of Craving.
 Additionally, Reap deals 10% increased damage.
The Season 2 Tier Set introduces a new proc, Soulburst, which
generates resources and deals damage. Following the mid-season tuning reducing

Note that this effect does also trigger from both Cull and

2.1. Devourer Demon Hunter Mechanics Breakdown
 Devourer is the newest specialization available to Demon Hunters, and
 has some very unique mechanics to make use of. Below, we'll be going over each
 of the key components, how they work, and help you understand why you're
 pressing each button.

3.1. Apex Talent: Midnight
Midnight has introduced Apex Talents, which are four-step talent nodes at the
bottom of your specialization tree, unlocking on the way from Level 80 to 90.
Uniquely, they're sequential nodes that add different effects with each extra
point invested. To access the next level of talents, you must put points
in the previous one, and can't just skip to the powerful end point. For Devourer,
the Apex talent focuses solely on Collapsing Star, greatly increasing its
damage!
 Point 1: Midnight - the first Apex node, causing your
 Collapsing Star to always critically strike. This greatly increases the
 damage of the ability, and has synergy with the already powerful talent
 Calamitous, further increasing the damage of your Collapsing Star.
 Points 2 & 3: Midnight - increases all Cosmic damage you
 deal with Devourer abilities by 3% per point. Additionally, it increases the
 Critical Strike damage of Collapsing Star by 50% of your Critical Strike
 chance per point, which is additive. For example, if you have 20%
 Critical Strike, your Collapsing Star will critically strike for 220%
 damage (or 260% damage with Calamitous).
 Point 4: Midnight - the most rotationally impactful node
 unlocked after investing all points. When taken, each time you enter
 Void Metamorphosis you will spawn five Soul Fragments, and immediately
 gain access to Collapsing Star. This effectively means a free star and
 8 Souls toward your second (due to the additional three from Star Fragments),
 while also ramping Impending Apocalypse.
 While the hero talents for Devourer don't have specific interactions with the
Apex Talents, they each have ways to leverage the up-front instant cast.
 Annihilator allows you to immediately trigger Dark Matter after
transforming, while Void-Scarred can use the quick access to reset
 The Hunt / Predator's Wake.
3.2. Devourer Resource Management
Devourer is a Dual Resource specialization, needing to balance
both its Fury and Soul Fragments as fuel for its abilities. Depending on whether
you are currently in Void Metamorphosis or not, they each serve a dual
function.
3.2.1. Fury
Fury is your primary resource while outside of Void Metamorphosis,
and is spent to cast Void Ray. This has a cap of 120 as Annihilator,
or 140 as Void-Scarred, and you should always avoid wasting any by
overcapping. This is generated by casting Consume,
 Voidblade / Hungering Slash, while Soul Immolation is
active, or by collecting Soul Fragments. Void Ray also generates 10 after its
channel completes via Voidglare Boon.
While inside Void Metamorphosis, your abilities no longer cost
Fury, and instead it passively drains while transformed. When you completely run
out, this effect ends, so generation is still crucial to maintaining the benefits of
your cooldown.
3.2.2. Soul Fragments
Souls grant 4 Fury when collected, and also fill up your secondary resource bar.
When you reach 50 (or 35 when playing Soul Glutton), you gain access to
your Void Metamorphosis form. While transformed, every 30 Souls generated
will also allow you to cast Collapsing Star once. Souls are
generated from a variety of abilities and talents, listed below:
 Consume spawns 1 Soul via Predator's Thirst
 Shattered Souls grants all ability damage a chance to spawn a Soul
 Entropy will passively spawn 1 Soul every 12 seconds
 Soul Immolation spawns 3 Souls over its duration
 Void Ray spawns a large number of Souls via Waste Not
 Collapsing Star spawns 3 Souls via Star Fragments
 Void Metamorphosis spawns 5 Souls on entry via Midnight
You can only have a maximum of 10 Souls on the ground at a time. Any after
this are automatically collected when a new one is spawned. Additionally, any Souls
collected after the limit of 50 while outside of Void Metamorphosis
(35 with Soul Glutton), or 40 while transformed, will be wasted. That
means you want to try to avoid collecting any until you've either activated your
window, or cast Collapsing Star when it becomes available.
3.2.3. Management Tips
Since both resources fluctuate in how important they are based on your
 Void Metamorphosis state, it can take a little bit of getting used to. A
few quick tips when getting started are:
 Don't Waste Fury or Souls - be aware of both caps, and always aim to
 stay below those limits. There's a small buffer for both, so make use of it.
 Check Before you Reap - bear in mind that Reap will collect
 Souls and generate 4 Fury for each, so consider holding off if it will significantly
 overcap your resources.
 Smuggle Souls - Souls are a very important resource, so try to have
 some present on the ground as you enter and leave Void Metamorphosis.
 Use Spenders Aggressively - generation of both resources can be very
 fast, so try to use Void Ray and Collapsing Star as soon as they
 activate.
3.3. Void Ray
 Void Ray is Devourer's primary spender outside of Void Metamorphosis,
and a key cooldown while inside it. This is the specialization equivalent to
Havoc's Eye Beam and Vengeance's Fel Devastation, boasting a
25-yard range. It can be channelled for up to 3 seconds (reduced by Haste),
and deals heavy Cosmic damage over the duration while rooting you in place.
Uniquely, it has a dual function depending on your current Void Metamorphosis state.
While outside of this form, it has no cooldown, but costs 100 Fury to cast.
While inside, it has a Hasted cooldown of 16 seconds (reduced to 14 by
 Voidpurge), and pauses your Fury drain both while channelling and
briefly after ending. Due to being a central part of Devourer, it has a number of
talents that augment it:
 Waste Not — significantly increases the rate of Souls it
 generates, turning it into one of your most reliable sources of Souls.
 Full Channel Rewards — four talent options add payoff to
 completing a channel. Voidglare Boon grants 10 Fury, Eradicate
 and Moment of Craving empower your follow-up Reap cast, and
 Final Breath increases the damage of the final tick by 300%.
 Damage Amplifiers — Focused Ray increases Void Ray
 damage by 80% when it strikes three targets or less, while Demonic Instinct
 increases all damage it deals by 10% per point.
Due to its raw damage alongside the effects tied to it, it is ideal to cast
 Void Ray as much as possible, ideally as soon as it becomes available. This
means avoiding Fury waste is especially important while outside of Void Metamorphosis,
as this directly leads to fewer Void Ray casts.
3.4. Melee Abilities
Uniquely, Devourer comes with a number of talent options that encourage
you to jump into melee range briefly for a few globals, before then quickly leaping back
out. Void-Scarred especially emphasizes this playstyle, with its core
talents being:
 Voidblade — 30-second cooldown ability that leaps to
 your target, dealing heavy Cosmic damage and generating Fury. Comes with a
 number of talents to empower it such as Duty Eternal,
 Devourer's Bite, Singular Strikes and Soulforged Blades.
 The Hunt — 90-second cooldown that charges to your target,
 dealing Cosmic damage and applying a DoT both to that target and up to 5 enemies
 in your path. Your hitbox remains active for a few seconds after this, allowing
 you to apply the DoT effect to any targets you missed.
 Hungering Slash — The core of the "melee combo". After
 using Voidblade or The Hunt, they are replaced on your
 action bar with this ability, causing you to slash in an arc around you. This
 shatters 2 Souls, generates 10 Fury, and can be further augmented by Soulforged Blades,
 Singular Strikes and Demonic Instinct. Additionally,
 when used, it triggers Voidstep, granting a free cast of
 Vengeful Retreat that deals Cosmic damage to nearby enemies when
 cast. This allows you to leap in, do the combo, and then quickly return to range.
 Voidrush — Adds extra value to Voidblade
 during Void Metamorphosis. This causes both it and The Hunt
 to briefly pause Fury drain when cast, and also reduces Voidblade's
 cooldown by 10 seconds after each Collapsing Star cast. This makes
 them extremely good for extending your transformations even further!
All of these talents combined create a strong incentive to mix in some quick
melee combos for a big payoff, especially for Void-Scarred. While
 Annihilator does not currently make use of it, they are still strong
independent talents.
3.4.1. Void-Scarred Synergy
 Void-Scarred in particular synergizes with the melee toolkit.
Upon entering your Void Metamorphosis form, all of these abilities are
upgraded - much like how Consume transforms into Devour:
 Voidblade becomes Pierce the Veil
 The Hunt becomes Predator's Wake
 Hungering Slash becomes Reaper's Toll
These upgraded forms all deal significantly more damage and, additionally, the
first cast of each ability after entering Void Metamorphosis will trigger
a Voidsurge for additional Cosmic damage. Finally, Void-Scarred
also has ways of increasing the amount of casts you have of each ability throughout
combat. Voidblade is reset upon entering Void Metamorphosis, and
 The Hunt is reset each time you cast Collapsing Star,
significantly increasing the number of casts available over the course of each
Metamorphosis window!
3.5. Reap Usage
 Reap is positioned as a central part of the Devourer
rotation. It has a base 8-second cooldown, instantly dealing Cosmic damage to your
target, and consuming up to 4 Soul Fragments. A variety of effects and talents
also modify it in different ways:
 Void Metamorphosis — Transforms Reap into
 Cull while active, significantly increasing its damage. Its cooldown is
 also reduced by 1 second each time you cast Devour (the replacement for
 Consume).
 Scythe's Embrace — Causes Reap to also
 generate 10 Fury.
 Second Helping — Grants Reap a second charge.
 Umbral Blade — Causes Reap's cooldown to be affected by Haste.
 Soulshaper & Moment of Craving — Soulshaper increases
 its damage dealt by 8% per Soul consumed. Moment of Craving resets Reap's
 cooldown, and allows your next Reap following a fully channelled Void Ray
 to consume up to 10 Souls. These effects serve as a large increase to Reap casts,
 and massively increase its damage.
 Eradicate — Upgrades Reap to Eradicate
 after fully channelling Void Ray. This causes it to deal damage in a
 25-yard cone in front of you, gaining all of the bonuses a standard Reap
 cast would. This is a very powerful AoE tool.
3.5.1. Void-Scarred Reap Synergy
For Void-Scarred, Reap is very powerful, and should be used
as much as possible. The Season 2 Tier Set further leans into this, and you should
aim to cast Reap (or Cull) in single-target whenever it will collect
four or more Soul Fragments. Eradicate follows a similar rule in AoE,
though ideally it consumes ten Souls instead.
3.5.2. Annihilator Synergy
 Annihilator hooks directly into Reap, using it as a way to
activate a number of its Hero Talents. The key effect - Voidfall - is
triggered via Consume / Devour casts and Void Ray.
After reaching 3 stacks, your next Reap will call down Void Meteors on your target.
Additionally, upon entering Void Metamorphosis, Annihilator
resets the cooldown of Reap, while also instantly generating 3 stacks
of Voidfall. This gives the tree strong up-front burst, while setting
up all of your important Hero tree effects to cover your key damage window.
For Annihilator, the tier set greatly empowers Reap, though
it already stands as a core part of the rotation. Similar to Void-Scarred,
you should aim to cast Reap (or Cull) in single-target whenever you have
four Soul Fragments active to collect, maximizing the number of Soulbursts
you trigger. Due to how much more frequently Reap is utilized in Season 2, there's
a lot less focus on manually triggering Voidfall, as it's often consumed
naturally in the rotation.
3.6. Soul Immolation
 Soul Immolation in almost all situations is played with
 Tempered Soul. While Soul Immolation itself isn't a powerful button, it
is a more powerful generator than Consume. It can also be cast
without a target, allowing you to generate extra Fury or Souls when moving between
pulls.
Bear in mind though, due to how much stronger Devour is, we do not
want to be using Soul Immolation inside of Void Metamorphosis. It
can also be used if needed for healing, but this should be considered as more of a
passive benefit, rather than something you are saving it for.

3.7. Devourer Demon Hunter Major Cooldowns
Devourer has a unique cooldown setup with Void Metamorphosis, limited
not by a timer but instead by resources. Using this is integral to the
playstyle, so in this section we'll be covering the mechanics behind it, giving you
a better understanding of how to execute these windows correctly. Due to the
amount of mechanics involved, we've separated the main ability from the key effects
it enables into different sections below:
4. Important Cooldowns for Devourer Demon Hunter
Void Metamorphosis
Collapsing Star
Rolling Torment
Metamorphosis Smuggling
4.1. Void Metamorphosis
Devourer's major cooldown is Void Metamorphosis. Unlike many other specs,
this does not have a fixed cooldown or duration, and is instead only
usable after collecting 50 Souls (reduced to 35 when playing Soul Glutton).
After use, it remains active until your Fury is drained to zero, and
while transformed you gain the following benefits:
 The benefits of Mastery: Monster Within are substantially increased.
 Consume becomes Devour, dealing significantly more damage and having a shorter cast time.
 Reap becomes Cull, dealing significantly more damage.
 Void Ray damage is significantly increased, and no longer costs Fury,
 instead having a Hasted cooldown. Also pauses Fury drain while channelling.
 You gain access to Collapsing Star after collecting 30 Souls.
 Your Fury bar is fully refilled.
 With Emptiness – You gain 0.25% Haste per Soul consumed.
 As Annihilator – You gain 3 stacks of Voidfall
 and refresh the cooldown of Reap.
 As Void-Scarred – The cooldowns of Voidblade and
 Soul Immolation are reset upon entry.
 As Void-Scarred – Voidblade, The Hunt
 and Hungering Slash are upgraded to Pierce the Veil, Predator's Wake
 and Reaper's Toll, respectively.
 As Void-Scarred – Collapsing Star resets the cooldown
 of The Hunt.
While in Void Metamorphosis, your Fury will drain at an increasingly
rapid pace, ramping up the difficulty of maintaining the form as time passes.
Thankfully, a handful of abilities will pause this drain briefly after
use: Void Ray and Collapsing Star, alongside Voidblade
and The Hunt when paired with the Voidrush talent.
Due to these factors, it's extremely important to plan around your
 Void Metamorphosis windows, ideally having some Souls already generated on
the ground to leverage after entry. While transformed, any Souls gathered
after 40 disappear, so avoid holding onto Collapsing Star casts for
too long if playing Annihilator.
4.2. Collapsing Star
While in your Void Metamorphosis form, all of your abilities deal
significantly more damage, but most importantly, you can cast your strongest
spell - Collapsing Star. This can only be cast while transformed, under
either of the following conditions:
You have gathered a total of 30 Soul Fragments.
On entry once via the Midnight Apex Talent.
When cast, this deals massive Cosmic damage to your target and nearby
enemies (reduced after 8 targets), and resets your Soul counter displayed on the
resource bar. Through the Impending Apocalypse talent, each subsequent
Star cast in each Void Metamorphosis will become 20% stronger; meaning
that the longer you can sustain your transformation the more powerful each cast
becomes.
Due to a plethora of buffs across the early portions of Season 2, as well as
the changes to the spec in 12.1.5, Devourer now makes significant use of
 Collapsing Star regardless of Hero Talents used. Annihilator
has the benefit of casting more Collapsing Stars per Void Metamorphosis
window, ramping its damage higher. Void-Scarred on the other hand uses
Collapsing Star to both reset the cooldown of The Hunt and provide
cooldown reduction for Voidblade. This makes it a core part of its melee
combo loop, and you want to cycle this combo as many times as possible during
each transformation.
4.3. Rolling Torment
 Rolling Torment serves as a redundancy tool for excess Souls gathered
while transformed. This rewards you with 5 Fury and 0.5% increased damage for 12
seconds per Soul present when your transformation expired. This means
it's less punishing to gather Souls without reaching a Collapsing Star
cast while in Void Metamorphosis, and also gives some nice resource
kickback. This is especially helpful in reaching your first Void Ray
after you leave, allowing you to restart your cycle faster.
The improvements to Collapsing Star over the course of Season 2, alongside
the quality of life benefits, now mean that Rolling Torment is a bit
healthier. Instead of warping the rotation around it, it's now a nice benefit to
help you cycle cooldowns more seamlessly.
4.4. Metamorphosis Smuggling
Smuggling is the act of carrying procs from outside of Void Metamorphosis
inside of it, leveraging the increased damage from both your Mastery, and the
increased damage Void Metamorphosis provides to abilities. This is primarily
done with a few abilities:
 Eradicate (in AoE).
 Hungering Slash.
 The Hunt.
In AoE, both hero talent trees look to always smuggle in an Eradicate,
due to the 30% upgrade Void Metamorphosis provides to it. This is a hard
rule, and the only ability you should be smuggling every time. For
 Void-Scarred, the other two abilities are important, but not to the degree
where you should go out of your way to achieve it. Smuggling a charge of
 Hungering Slash upgrades it into Reaper's Toll, and is fairly easy
to achieve by casting Voidblade right before activating
Void Metamorphosis.
For The Hunt, things are a bit more involved, using a technique
referred to as Huggling (Hunt Smuggling). To Huggle, a baseline level of roughly
15% Haste (although Devourer aims for more) is required, alongside a minimum
distance of 15 yards from your target. The easiest way to set this up is by using
 Vengeful Retreat right before casting The Hunt,
then, as you charge to your target, activate Void Metamorphosis. This
triggers the form switch in the middle of the charge, resulting in the damage
event switching to Predator's Wake instead, both increasing its damage and
granting you a charge of Reaper's Toll. This is fairly easy to do with a
little practice, though the animation locks may make it hard to notice when you've
pulled it off.
Note though, while smuggling is important for Void-Scarred when trying
to optimize for the maximum throughput possible, cycling through Void Metamorphosis
is still more powerful. Don't delay the activation for any meaningful amount of
time to smuggle buffs over, aside from Eradicate in AoE.
4.5. Managing Devourer Demon Hunter Movement
Unlike its melee equivalent spec Havoc, Devourer is a caster, so it has
unique challenges when navigating an encounter. Due to its mix of 25-yard range
spells and melee abilities, knowing what's at your disposal is very
important to getting the most out of it. Thankfully, Devourer comes with
some excellent movement tools:
 Consume and Reap – Consume / Devour
 is your main filler spell, and can be cast while on the move at all times. This
 means you can always cast something at any point in a given encounter.
 Reap is also instant, giving you some options!
 Shift – Unique to Devourer, this is a 20-second cooldown
 targeted dash with up to 3 charges, depending on talents. It's near-instant,
 moving you to your cursor anywhere within 30 yards, including up terrain to gain
 height. Importantly though, this is not a teleport - meaning that
 you will still be hit by effects you pass through on your way.
 Glide and Vengeful Retreat – If you've ever played
 Demon Hunter before, these should be familiar! Vengeful Retreat
 quickly launches you backward 20 yards, with a 25-second cooldown. While used in
 the melee combo in combat, this comes with free uses, so don't be afraid to use
 it if needed. Glide can be used at any time after double jumping,
 providing a small movement speed boost. When paired with Wings of Wrath
 in Midnight, this becomes an extremely efficient movement tool that's always
 available.
When combined, these tools create an extremely robust movement package. This is
especially important for Devourer, as your main damaging abilities in both
 Void Ray and Collapsing Star have long cast times, while also
being interrupted if you try to move. Thus, it's crucial to use other tools such
as Shift to move to a safe spot, giving you room to unleash your most
potent spells!
