BEST DEVOURER DEMON HUNTER MYTHIC+ TALENT BUILDS
Devourer in AoE is built entirely around the incredibly strength of  Eradicate and  Collapsing Star. Each hero talent augments this damage in additional ways.  Annihilator brings large damage from  Collapsing Star and it's signature passive,  Voidfall, whereas  Void-Scarred focuses on leveraging resets on it's abilities from  Collapsing Star, casting more  The Hunt.
Annihilator M+
Void-Scarred M+
Annihilator is all about casting large  Collapsing Stars in Mythic+, and plays very similar to its raid counterpart build. Focus on your  Eradicate with this build, and don't waste  Voidfall procs. This build is recommended for the current season.

BEST DEVOURER DEMON HUNTER DELVE TALENT BUILD
The Devourer Demon Hunter Delve build is similar to the older variations of  Void-Scarred and is suited to the quick, frequent pulls that Delves offer you.
Void-Scarred Delve
This build plays around the melee talents of  Void-Scarred, while also taking  Eradicate for more cleave, and  Devourer's Bite for single and priority target damage! It now seeks to cast  Collapsing Star, aiming to get 2 every Void Metamorphosis window!
