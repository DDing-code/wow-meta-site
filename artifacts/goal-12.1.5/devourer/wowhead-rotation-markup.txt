Devourer Demon Hunter Rotation in Midnight Season 2
Devourer Demon Hunter uses two separate resources and features a different cadence to their rotation depending on whether you are inside {1217607} or not. This can seem overwhelming at first, but the spec has some core concepts to help follow and keep things on track. The core concepts to keep in mind in single target are these, in order of importance:
[table style=grid]
[tr][td]
[table]
 [tr]
 [td width=6%][/td]
 [td]Avoid Resource Waste: Use {1217607} and {473728} as soon as they are available. To take full advantage of our tier set, look to use {1226019} whenever it will collect 4 or more souls.
 [/td][/tr]
[/table]
[hr]
[table]
 [tr]
 [td width=6%][/td]
 [td]Plan Cooldown Usage: The goals of your {1217607} windows will be to cast as many {1221167}s as possible. [symbol=wow-hero-talent-annihilator] Annihilator seeks to cast 4-5 in Single Target, and 5+ in AoE, while [symbol=wow-hero-talent-void-scarred] Void-Scarred seeks to cast 3-4 of them In Single-Target, and 4+ in AoE. Either way, a lot of your damage comes from these windows, so ensure minimal disruption from them.
 [/td][/tr]
[/table]
[hr]
[table]
 [tr]
 [td width=6%][/td]
 [td]Accelerate Your Rotation: {1226019} is used to accelerate into {473728} and {1217607}, collecting souls, generating Fury and dealing a large amount of your damage. In season 2, both hero talent trees are looking to cast it as much as possible in Single Target, using it whenever it will collect 4+ souls (10 for {1239524}), to take advantage of our tier set.[/td]
 [/tr]
[/table]
[/td][/tr][/table]

 Best Devourer Demon Hunter Opener

Instead, Devourer's opener is setup to get to Void Metamorphosis as quickly as possible, before then starting to do your damage. [tab name="Void Metamorphosis" icon=inv_112_ability_demonhunter_metamorphasisvoid]
 Void Metamorphosis‎ 
{1217607} is your main damaging cooldown as a Devourer Demon Hunter, transforming you into a Void Empowered Demon form, and empowering your character in a variety of ways. 
Unlike most specializations, {1217607} does not have a cooldown or a duration. Instead of a cooldown, it is available when you collect 50 Soul Fragments (or 35 Soul Fragments if you have the talent {1247534}). Due to the talent {1261684}, we start combat with 25 Soul Fragments already collected, so a small period of ramp is needed before we have access to our cooldown. While inside of {1217607}, its duration is dependent on your Fury bar. Your Fury bar is instantly refilled when you enter and drains over time, ramping in drain intensity over time. Some of your abilities will briefly pause this drain, which we outline below. 
While in Void Metamorphosis, the following benefits apply:
[li] Your Fury bar is refilled, and drains over time, increasing in drain speed the longer you are in Void Metamorphosis. Once your Fury bar reaches 0, you drop out of Void Metamorphosis.[/li]
[li] Your Mastery increases in power [/li]
[li]{473662} is upgraded into {1217610}, dealing more damage, having a shorter cast time, and reducing the cooldown of {1226019} by 1 sec per cast[/li]
[li]{1226019} is upgraded into {1245453}, dealing more damage[/li]
[li]{473728} is upgraded, dealing more damage, costing no fury, and pausing your Fury drain while channeling it. It also gains a 14s hasted cooldown while inside of Void Metamorphosis. This cooldown does not persist outside of Void Metamorphosis [/li]
[li] You gain access to the powerful spell {1221167} [/li]
[li] While inside of Void Metamorphosis, the following spells will briefly pause your fury drain:
 [li] {473728} [/li]
 [li] {1221167} [/li]
 [li] {1245412} with the talent {1272422} [/li]
 [li] {1246167} with the talent {1272422} [/li]
[/li]
[li] [symbol=wow-hero-talent-annihilator] Annihilator resets the cooldown of {1226019}, and automatically proc {1256301}. [/li]
[li] [symbol=wow-hero-talent-void-scarred] Void-Scarred resets the cooldown {1241937} and {1245412}. Additionally, your abilities are further upgraded: 
 [li]{1245412} is upgraded into {1245483}[/li]
 [li]{1239519} is upgraded into {1245470}[/li]
 [li]{1246167} is upgraded into {1259431}[/li]
 Furthermore, the first cast of each melee ability triggers a {1246160} [/li]
[li] Each cast of {1221167} resets the cooldown of {1246167}. [/li]
[hr]
Both Hero Trees seek to extend this form as long as possible, casting as many {1221167}s as possible to leverage the powerful damage and bonuses it provides.

 Collapsing Star 
While not technically a cooldown in the traditional sense, {1221167} is quite important for [symbol=wow-hero-talent-annihilator] Annihilator at all target counts. {1221167} is only accessible inside of {1217607}, and requires the collection of 30 Soul Fragments to be able to cast it. This spell deals a high amount of Single Target and AoE damage, and you should aim to cast as many as possible while inside of {1217607}. 
[hr]
A tip to do this is known as Soul Smuggling. Soul Smuggling is the technique of leaving Soul Fragments on the ground when you go into or out of {1217607}. By doing this, you are effectively "smuggling" those souls, allowing them to carry over and increase the speed to which you can reach your next {1221167} or {1217607}. 
[hr]
For [symbol=wow-hero-talent-annihilator] Annihilator, {1221167} is especially important due to {1253817} and {1256307}. As [symbol=wow-hero-talent-annihilator] Annihilator, you should aim to cast at least 5 {1221167} per {1217607}.
[hr]
For [symbol=wow-hero-talent-void-scarred] Void-Scarred, {1221167} is also important due to the 12.1.5 changes. Each cast reduces the cooldown of {1245412} by 10s, and resets the cooldown of {1246167} due to {452415}. As such, aim to cast both of these before casting Collapsing Star, and try to cast Collapsing Star 3-4 times in ST, and 4+ in AoE

[tab name="Smuggling and Huggling" icon=inv_helmet_66]
 Smuggling 
 If you've been around the Devourer Demon Hunter community for any length of time, you may have heard of an action called smuggling. While this is NOT related to real life smuggling (this guide doesn't teach that, sorry), what it does describe is the action of taking a proc or resource into or out of {1217607} where you otherwise wouldn't be able to. Generally speaking, Smuggling is often done with the following things:
[li]{1227619}[/li]
[li]{1239519}[/li]
[li]{1226033} and {1238488}[/li]
[li] {1246167}[/li]
[pad]
Let's breakdown what Smuggling refers to in each of these cases, and why you'd want to do it!
Shattered Souls
Soul Fragments serve as the most important resource for Devourer, powering you to access your key cooldown in {1217607} while in normal form, or allowing you to cast the powerful {1221167} when already inside of it. Soul Fragments for Devourer exist in two separate states, those collected outside of Void Metamorphosis, and those collected while already inside of it. Importantly, these stacks do not carry over when you change forms, and are wiped each time you enter or leave Void Metamorphosis. 
Thankfully, Soul Fragments do not automatically pick up, and we can have 10 lingering on the ground at any given time without collecting them. Because of these, we are able to leave Soul Fragments on the ground when we enter or leave Void Metamorphosis, and then collect them after. This allows us to enter Void Meta with more progress made towards Collapsing Star, or to leave Void Meta and have some progress made to get into our next one faster! 
Hungering Slash
[pad]
By carrying any {1239519} proc we have outside of {1217607} into it, it upgrades into {1245470}, gaining 20% additional damage. On top of that, our damage further increases in Void Metamorphosis due to the second half of our mastery {1238855}, further increasing our damage. This allows us to get 3 supercharged {1245470} per Void Metamorphosis, up from 2. 
While this is often done by utilizing {1245412} prior to entering Void Metamorphosis, it can also be done with {1246167}. This is often referred to as "Huggling" and has its own dedicated section down below. 
Eradicate and Moment of Craving
[pad]
Similar to smuggling {1239519}, by smuggling {1226033} and {1238488} into {1217607}, we're able to take advantage of the 30% more damage that {1245453} gains, as well as the additional damage we gain from our mastery {1238855}. By smuggling {1238488} into Void Meta, we're able to spend that on a Cull rather than a {1226019}. Likewise, since {473728} is reset upon entering Void Meta, we're able to use 2 Moment of Craving buffed Culls at the start of our burst, rather than 1. {1226033} works similarly, upgrading into the "Cull" version when entering Meta, and doing 30% more damage (+damage from our mastery) as a result. While this behaviour is not currently used in Single Target, in AOE, we're aiming to smuggle an Eradicate into Void Meta every time that we are able to. 
Huggling
Newly discovered in S2 of Midnight, Huggling (Hunt Smuggling) is a turbo charged version of smuggling in a {1239519}. By pressing {1217607} during the travel time of The Hunt, we are able to activate our Meta Form before The Hunt deals damage, upgrading it into {1259431} prior to damage being dealt. This means we gain the upgraded damage on The Hunt, and are still able to smuggle in Hungering Slash. 
Performing this trick is fairly easy, requiring roughly 14% haste and enough distance so your gcd comes up during The Hunt travel. The easiest way to do this, and the way we recommend, is to {1245412} followed by {198793}. While midair from the Vengeful Retreat, cast The Hunt, and then press Void Metamorphosis as you travel towards your target. The animation will get locked out until you finish the charge, so it will look like nothing happened, but it *will* successfully Huggle. 
This trick is not very useful in AoE, following the changes in 12.1.5. However, we aim to do it every time we can in Single-Target.
[/tab]
[tab name="Wiggling" icon=ability_seal]
 Wiggling
 Wiggling is another Devourer Demon Hunter specific behavior, due to one of our resources, Souls, existing on the ground. Wiggling refers to the behavior of moving slightly back and forth while casting, with the intention to pick up some soul fragments from the ground. While this is normally incorrect to do, as you will automatically collect any souls on the ground above 10, and we want {1226019} to collect as many as possible due to {1238739}, there are situations where it's worthwhile to try and wiggle. Those are:
[ol]
[li] Less than 10 fury is needed to cast {473728}.[/li]
[li] Less than 4 souls are needed to cast {1221167} and {1226019} is on cooldown.[/li]
[li] You are very late into a {471306} window, and collecting souls will allow you to cast another {1221167}.[/li]
[/ol]
In any of these situations, strafing left or right while casting {473662} will allow you to pick up a few soul fragments, and greatly accelerate your rotation! 
[/tab]
[tab name="Shipping" icon=inv_10_engineering2_boxofbombs_friendly_color3]
Shipping
Conversly to smuggling, which is the act of bringing things into {1217607}, Shipping is where we bring things out of Void Meta (since its legal get it). 
This is primarly done with 2 things, and only really done in [symbol=wow-hero-talent-void-scarred] Void-Scarred AoE 
[li]{1217610} buffed with {1297432}.[/li]
[li]{1226033}.[/li]
We aim to ship these out as they allow us to much quicker cycle through to our next Void Meta, rather than extend the tail end of our current one. Because of powerful passives like {452414}, {452410}, and {1244237}, our damage remains high outside of Void Meta. Thus, by shipping these spells out, they retain high damage, but also provide 10-20 souls towards our next Void Meta, allowing us to cycle through our cooldowns significantly faster than if we used them at the end of our previous one!
[/tab]

Reap Usage
Following the changes to our season two tier set, {1226019} has now mostly fallen out of the rotation for both Hero Trees, assuming you are facing 2 or more targets. It's use case varies depending on the target count and the selected Hero Tree, all of which are outlined below. 
Single Target: In Single Target, Reap is used at 4+ souls for BOTH Hero Talent trees, due to its strong damage.
[symbol=wow-hero-talent-void-scarred] Void-Scarred 2+ Targets: While fighting 2+ targets as [symbol=wow-hero-talent-void-scarred] Void-Scarred, Reap is essentially never used as it robs souls from {1239524}, making it harder to use this at 10+ Souls. While you can occasionally find places to weave a Reap in when you get lucky with procs, we generally do not recommend doing so as it will be a sizeable loss of AoE damage if you do starve Eradicate. If the priority damage is important, you can use Reap or Cull for more of it. 
[symbol=wow-hero-talent-annihilator] Annihilator 2+ Targets: While fighting 2+ targets as [symbol=wow-hero-talent-annihilator] Annihilator, we go back to a season one rotational style, only using Reap if it will proc {1256302}, or give you enough souls to cast {1221167}. Always aim to cast {1239524} at 10 souls whenever possible.
