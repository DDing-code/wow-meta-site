## Marksmanship Hunter Overview
Marksmanship Hunter is a relatively mobile ranged DPS spec that is focused on slow-casting, but hard-hitting cooldown-limited abilities like Aimed Shot and Rapid Fire. Marksmanship Hunters are damage-focused snipers with excellent burst damage, mobility, and flexibility.
Damage Raid Mythic+ Utility Survivability Mobility
Strong Cleave, Steep Drop-Off
Marksmanship is excellent at single-target raid cleave, delivering powerful burst on 1-4 targets. The more targets you add, the worse it gets, and its damage drops off a cliff once you go past 8 targets due to its hard target cap, which is bad for Mythic+.
Excellent for this Raid
Marksmanship has excellent single-target, 2-4-target cleave, burst, and execute. Almost all things that are very important in this raid, making it one of the best raiding specs.
Capped AoE, Weak Priority Damage
Marksmanship's damage gets worse the more targets you add, and it drops off a cliff once you go past 8 targets, which is common in Mythic+. Even at exactly 8 targets, where the cap should make it strongest, its AoE is not ahead of the other specs. Its single-target and low-target cleave are solid, but its priority damage is awful, and large pulls are where Mythic+ is played, making it one of the weaker specs for general Mythic+.
Unremarkable
We bring mediocre utility to the group. A 1-minute stun/AoE stop in Intimidation, and a regular cooldown ranged kick. The flexibility and cooldown of our AoE stops are mediocre. Hunter's Mark is mandatory in raids, but it is not particularly strong in Mythic+.
Strong Active Defenses, Weak Passives
Marksmanship has access to several strong active defensive abilities, allowing it to mitigate heavy damage effectively. However, its passive defenses are relatively weak, making it reliant on timing and proper use of cooldowns to survive in high-damage situations. It suffers in "rot" damage situations, with constantly ticking hard DoTs. As a result, Hunters often require more external healing than other classes, and the skill ceiling is relatively high, while the skill floor is relatively low.
High potential, but high skill floor
While Marksmanship spends around 40% of its time casting spells that render you immobile, it is reasonably flexible in when you cast these spells. Because Aimed Shot has two charges, it can be delayed while you are moving, and as long as you never cap on charges, you are not losing anything. Taking advantage of this can make Marksmanship a fairly mobile spec in practice.

## Class Changes
Patch 12.1.5 removes Unload and puts a new talent, Blood Fletching, in its place. Practically speaking, the primary difference is that Rapid Fire no longer spends Precise Shots for you, so as Sentinel you once again need to spend the proc before you cast Rapid Fire like in Season 1, unless Trueshot is up, while as Dark Ranger you simply cast Rapid Fire on cooldown. Blood Fletching changes nothing about your rotation and is a purely passive benefit.
  - New Talent: Blood Fletching. Aimed Shot critical strikes now cause the Master Marksman bleed at 20% of the damage dealt instead of 10%. For 4 seconds after a critical Aimed Shot, the target also takes extra bleed damage every time one of your abilities damages it.
  - Unload has been removed. Rapid Fire no longer fires an Arcane Shot at the start and end of its channel, so it no longer spends Precise Shots on its own. This has significant rotational implications which are detailed on our Rotation page.
Our Rotation page has been updated for Patch 12.1.5. On our Gear page, the Catalyzed Aberrant Commander's Gauntlets from the Kith'ix raid are our new BiS Gloves, and there is a new list of what to spend your first Ascendant Venomstones on.

## Class Changes
Marksmanship got by far the largest rework of the three Hunter specializations in Season 2. Explosive Shot is back as a wholly different spell, Rapid Fire deals significant damage again while Aimed Shot has been nerfed.
The Omnium Folio borrowed-power system carries into Season 2, and it affects both your talent choices and your gear. You can find the details on our dedicated Talents and Gear Guides.
  - Explosive Shot now lasts for 3 seconds, and every 1 second the target explodes for Fire damage, dealing 50% of that damage to all enemies within 8 yards. Deals reduced damage beyond 5 targets.
  - New Talent: Unstable Trigger. Explosive Shot can now be used a second time if cast within 3 seconds, and it incorporates any remaining damage from previous Explosive Shots on your target. It is a choice node with Tactical Reload.
  - Shrapnel Shot is now Tactical Reload, and causes Explosive Shot to grant Lock and Load.
  - Incendiary Ammunition is now Accuracy By Volume, and makes Bulletstorm affect 1 additional Aimed Shot.
  - Precision Detonation now makes Explosive Shot last 1 second longer, which means it will explode for an extra tick of damage.
  - Bulletstorm now makes Rapid Fire increase the damage of your next Aimed Shot by 20%.
  - Windrunner Quiver now increases Rapid Fire damage by 10% and gives it a 30% chance to grant Lock and Load.
  - Deathblow now gives Aimed Shot a 10% chance to cause your next Aimed Shot to grant Deathblow.
  - Aspect of the Hydra no longer affects Arcane Shot.
  - The following talents have been removed: Double Tap and Headshot.
  - Eagle's Accuracy is now a 1-point talent that increases the damage of Aimed Shot by 5% (was 10%) and Rapid Fire by 10% (was 20%). Focused Aim now reduces the cooldown of Aimed Shot by 1 second (was 2 seconds). Small Game Hunter is now a 2-point talent.
  - Some talents moved in the second gate of the tree to make Explosive Shot easier to obtain, and the Tenacious/ Cunning node is no longer required pathing to reach Trueshot.
  - Apex Talents: Take Aim (Rank 2) now increases the critical damage of your abilities by 5%/10% and the damage of your ranged abilities by 3%/6%. Take Aim (Rank 3) now causes Spotter's Mark to also increase the damage of your next Rapid Fire against the target by 20%, on top of the Aimed Shot bonus.
  - Dark Ranger: Through the Eyes has been redesigned to a flat 10% Kill Shot and Black Arrow damage increase, and it now correctly increases Withering Fire's damage
  - Dark Ranger: Black Arrow no longer highlights itself when usable
  - Dark Ranger: Black Arrow's direct damage was reduced by 40%, then increased by 25%
  - Dark Ranger: Bleak Arrows damage increased by 300%, and Withering Fire's Black Arrow damage reduced by 40%
  - Dark Ranger: Dark Minion summons can now be tracked on the Cooldown Manager.
  - Sentinel: Moon's Blessing now only causes 1 second of cooldown reduction to Aimed Shot (was 2 seconds). Sentinel's Mark duration increased to 16 seconds (was 12 seconds).
  - Tuning: Aimed Shot, Rapid Fire, Arcane Shot, Multi-Shot and Kill Shot damage were all increased by 20%, with further passes since: Rapid Fire +20%, Arcane Shot +15%, Kill Shot +25%, Steady Shot +50%, Volley +20%, Multi-Shot +80% then -20%, and all damage +5%. Auto Shot damage increased by 20%, and ranged auto-shot damage increased by 600%. Explosive Shot was retuned twice after its redesign, by 15% and then by 100%, and it now only explodes for 50% of its damage to other nearby enemies.
  - Tier Set: 2-Set: Explosive Shot lasts 1 second longer and deals 5% increased damage. 4-Set: Aimed Shot and Rapid Fire damage increased by 5%. Each time Explosive Shot causes a burst of damage, the cooldown of Aimed Shot and Rapid Fire is reduced by 0.5 seconds.
  - Bug Fixes: Small Game Hunter now correctly increases Explosive Shot's damage. Explosive Shot's area damage no longer benefits twice from Mastery, critical damage bonuses and Take Aim, no longer hits its own primary target, and still goes off if the primary target dies to it. Deathblow now procs at the expected rate from Aimed Shot, and Aspect of the Hydra no longer blocks or consumes those procs.

## Marksmanship Hunter Changes in Midnight
The Midnight Expansion brings huge changes for all Hunter specs, primarily focused on streamlining and simplifying aspects of the kit.
2.6.1.
## Dark Ranger
  - No more Deathblow procs from Auto-Shots.
  - Rapid Fire is now guaranteed to grant Deathblow.
  - After you activate Trueshot, the Trueshot button itself turns into Wailing Arrow, a powerful cast that activates Deathblow and also kicks/silences all targets hit for 1 sec.
2.6.2.
## Sentinel
  - Spotter's Mark is replaced by Sentinel's Mark and is much stronger.
  - Don't Look Back is now a flat 10% absorb shield that you get whenever you get a Sentinel's Mark proc.
  - Maximum Focus increased to 125.
  - Like Dark Ranger, after activating Trueshot, the Trueshot button becomes Moonlight Chakram which sends a glowy chakram that deals heavy AoE damage as it bounces between enemies. A choice node can cause this Chakram to give us Lock and Load.
2.7.
## DPS & Gameplay Changes
  - Streamline is gone and as a result, Aimed Shot's cast time is much longer at a baseline of 2.5 sec. During Trueshot, this is reduced to 1.5 sec. Its damage has been buffed to compensate, but overall, expect a slower-playing, more sniper-focused and deliberate Marksmanship "feel" in Midnight.
  - All of the Explosive Shot-related Talents are gone. We do keep Tactical Reload, which now triggers off Volley.
  - Other removed talents include Razor Fragments, Moving Target, and of course, Improved Streamline.
2.8.
## Apex Talents
Midnight introduces new Apex Talents, unlocked between Level 80 and 90. Marksmanship receives:
  - Take Aim
  - Take Aim
  - Take Aim
They cause Rapid Fire to reduce Aimed Shot's cooldown by 5 sec over its channel.
2.9.
## Survivability Changes
  - Hunters have +2% higher max HP than previously.
  - Exhilaration's cooldown is now a 1-minute flat, instead of variable (and often lower).
  - Aspect of the Turtle's cooldown is now 2:00 with talents, down from 2:30.
  - Aspect of the Turtle can now be a 50% damage reduction with talents, up from 30%.
  - Roar of Sacrifice is now an external defensive that can be used on yourself or any friendly target to reduce their damage taken by 15% for 10 sec, but 50% of their damage taken will be taken by your pet. For Marksmanship, the pet part just disappears if you have no pet.
  - Fortitude of the Bear is now 3% passive damage reduction, instead of being an active defensive cooldown.
2.10.
## Utility Changes
  - Hunter's Mark is now a universal 3% damage increase against a single-target. This essentially guarantees Hunter a raid spot in every raid. It is also a boost to the group in Mythic+, though it is much less potent there.
  - Counter Shot now spell-locks the target for 5 seconds, up from 3.
  - Freezing Trap can now slow the target by 70% for 4 sec after the trap breaks.
  - Intimidation is now our sole stun/stop. A choice node either reduces its cooldown to 40 sec, or makes it a 1-second AoE stun. Implosive Trap, High Explosive Trap, and Bursting Shot are all gone.
  - The slows from Wing Clip and Concussive Shot are 25% and 10% stronger, respectively.
  - Horsehair Tether is now a Class Tree node and not attached to the Pack Leader Hero Talent. It is a choice node with Tar-Coated Bindings, which extends Binding Shot's stun by 1 sec.
  - Ghillie Suit is gone.
  - Scatter Shot is gone.
  - Scrappy is gone.
  - Binding Shackles is gone.
2.11.
## Miscellaneous Changes
  - Aspect of the Cheetah's initial "super" speed burst is now twice as long with talents.
  - Kill Shot is now exclusive to the Marksmanship tree.
  - Successfully kicking with Counter Shot/ Muzzle now grants 30 Focus, up from 10, but soothing/purging with Tranquilizing Shot no longer grants Focus.
Overall, we are gaining defensiveness and mobility, while in the utility department, we are losing some things (like the ability to move mobs) but gaining others (stronger Mark, streamlined AoE stun, stronger kick and more Focus return on kicking). We think the changes are broadly positive!

## Basics of Marksmanship Hunter Gameplay
Marksmanship Hunter is a spec about cooldown management, uptime, and flow.
You want to cast your hard-hitter abilities like Aimed Shot, Rapid Fire, and Kill Shot as often as possible. In-between these abilities, which all have independent cooldowns, you are managing your Focus by using Arcane Shot as a spender and Steady Shot as a filler and builder. When you cast Aimed Shot, your next Arcane Shot/ Multi-Shot will do significantly increased damage, and so you have a fairly consistent flow where a Precise Shots-generator is immediately followed by a spender.
During Trueshot, your exciting main cooldown, everything dramatically speeds up and you will be spamming both Aimed Shot and Rapid Fire with their reduced cooldown. The main benefit conferred by this cooldown is significantly increased Crit Chance (more Crits) and Crit Damage (stronger Crits). Crit is a huge stat for Marksmanship due to these and other mechanics.
On AoE, you want to activate the Trick Shots buff for all of your Aimed and Rapid Fire casts. This is done by simply weaving at least one Multi-Shot in-between every single cast of Aimed Shot and Rapid Fire, even during Trueshot.
The best Marksmanship Hunters are excellent at doing these things on the fly and during hectic movement (which prevents Aimed Shot casting) and mechanics. They always know what to cast next and they have a great awareness of their Focus levels and the status of their cooldowns at all times. To condense things further, Marksmanship can be described briefly with the following bullet points.
  - Spend All of Your Precise Shots
  Every single Aimed Shot and Rapid Fire is going to grant Precise Shots, and we want to spend all of these Precise Shots procs before we cast another Aimed Shot. Do not cast Aimed Shot if you have a Precise Shots unspent. As Sentinel, spend them before Rapid Fire too, unless Trueshot is up.
  - Always be Casting
  Casting as many abilities as possible matters more than pressing the perfect button every time, and dead time is the single biggest thing holding most players back from their DPS goals. Steady Shot is how you fill it, and it is castable while moving, so there is no excuse for a dead global.
