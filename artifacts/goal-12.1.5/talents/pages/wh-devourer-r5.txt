BEST DEVOURER DEMON HUNTER MYTHIC+ TALENT BUILDS
Devourer in AoE is built entirely around the incredibly strength of  박멸 and  붕괴하는 별. Each hero talent augments this damage in additional ways.  Annihilator brings large damage from  붕괴하는 별 and it's signature passive,  공허내림, whereas  Void-Scarred focuses on leveraging resets on it's abilities from  붕괴하는 별, casting more  사냥.
Annihilator M+
Void-Scarred M+
Annihilator is all about casting large  붕괴하는 별s in Mythic+, and plays very similar to its raid counterpart build. Focus on your  박멸 with this build, and don't waste  공허내림 procs. This build is recommended for the current season.

BEST DEVOURER DEMON HUNTER DELVE TALENT BUILD
The Devourer Demon Hunter Delve build is similar to the older variations of  Void-Scarred and is suited to the quick, frequent pulls that Delves offer you.
Void-Scarred Delve
This build plays around the melee talents of  Void-Scarred, while also taking  박멸 for more cleave, and  포식자의 이빨 for single and priority target damage! It now seeks to cast  붕괴하는 별, aiming to get 2 every Void Metamorphosis window!
