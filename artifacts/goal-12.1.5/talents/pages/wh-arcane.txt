Below, I have provided a list of import codes for the strongest Arcane Mage builds for Midnight Season 2. Keep in mind that research is endless and so things may change over time.

With the current tuning,  Sunfury is simply the best choice in all content, with more damage than  Spellslinger in all content.  Spellslinger has another weakness as well, which is its' reliance on  비전 보주, a spell that historically has a problem once an expansion at least.  비전 보주s can fall into holes in floors, it can fail to hit elevated targets, especially elevated targets that are in holes in the floor, Blizzard sometimes catches these issues, but as the first example that comes to mind: 간수의 눈 which could not be hit by Orbs at all, it is an important consideration when it comes up.  Spellslinger also has a significantly weaker defensive node, meaning there really isn't much reason to play  Spellslinger except that its more purple.
BEST ARCANE MAGE RAID TALENT BUILD
Sunfury
This is the most standard build for Arcane Mage, you spend Salvo/Charges on Barrage, build them back with your other spells.
Cleave/AOE
To enable the below single target build to do AOE is simple. Swapping out  자극,  비전 전령, and  충만한 전령 for  공명 and both points of  에테르 조율 is an easy tradeoff for  Sunfury and quite a bit more difficult for  Spellslinger who also needs to consider trading off  고전압 for  보주 숙련 as well as how to handle losing  비전 전령 talents in order to deal maximum AOE - which is currently still worse than  Sunfury.
