BEST UNHOLY DEATH KNIGHT MYTHIC+ TALENT BUILDS
When it comes to Mythic+, Unholy currently has 2 viable buids. One for  San'layn and one for  Rider of the Apocalypse. More detailed explanations of the builds will be in their respective tabs.
Rider of the Apocalypse
San'layn
For  Rider of the Apocalypse in Mythic+, this build plays almost exactly like Unholy normally does in Single Target scenarios. The only major difference is swapping to pressing Epidemic instead of Death Coil when fighting 3+ targets. This build mostly focuses on buffing our Magus' as much as possible, and summoning as many as possible to deal most of our sustained AoE damage.
