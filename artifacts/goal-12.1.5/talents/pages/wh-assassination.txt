Venomous Abyss Talents
 Deathstalker finds itself being roughly 2-5% stronger than  Fatebound in all content.  Deathstalker is, however, much harder to play and can be frustrating to manage the associated debuff. Deathstalker also requires several extra buttons in Single Target as part of its rotation, adding even more complexity. Often, Deathstalker isn't worth the hassle and performs worse.

An important note for Mythic+ is that top players will tend to gravitate towards  Deathstalker for the increased funnel damage from  하나 된 집중력 and the extra defensive layer, regardless of damage output. For most players who will not be running the highest-level keys, it is generally recommended to play  Fatebound instead, as its improved quality of life will often outweigh the benefits of  Deathstalker. This minor damage loss is rarely the reason keys aren't timed. If you are playing above the 1% title range cutoff, this added funnel might be necessary.

It should also be noted that the added funnel/priority damage added by  Deathstalker is not an intrinsic gain by itself. Tank grouping, the route, group composition, and more heavily influence whether you can leverage  Deathstalker's strengths. For example, if a tank doesn't pull additional mobs onto a boss,  Deathstalker loses a lot of value. Keep this in mind for your dungeon runs, especially when pugging.
BEST ASSASSINATION ROGUE RAID TALENT BUILD
Raid builds for Assassination have a surprising amount of choice, but will generally funnel into one of two buckets: a general raid build that takes our AoE suite of talents, or a full single-target build that avoids them. Trying to go somewhere in-between is possible, but typically only serves to hinder the single-target potential of the spec while not providing a significant gain in AoE.

With the variance in fights and the difficulties uniquely facing your own raid group, it is encouraged to experiment with your talents to better deal with those challenges.
Fatebound
Deathstalker
 Fatebound is between 2-3% behind  Deathstalker across the raid, but it's easier, more forgiving of mistakes, and has better quality of life. For the vast majority of players,  Fatebound will simply perform better.

The talents below are a sample mixture of Cleave and Single Target that should serve well enough for most situations. On a lot of the Mythic encounters in the raid, talents like  피의 향기 can be preferred and are discussed below.

More AoE Talents
Sometimes  피의 향기 value can be incredibly impactful to a fight, and should be opted into. The breakpoint where this generally becomes worth it is between 2 and 3 targets. Something like the Lost Explorers encounter, where players can spread bleeds between two targets and an occasional third, is a perfect opportunity to take more AoE talents.

I would also recommend taking  정확한 투척술 in any cleave situation. We are pressing Fan of Knives in 2-target situations, which means that we are getting immense value from Thrown Precision.  날아가는 단검 can be taken as a comfort pick for more spread targets, but can be swapped out interchangeably for  치명적인 혼합물 instead, which should do more damage. In some encounters,  갑작스러운 최후 can be worth considering in place of the last Apex Point. This is discussed further below.

Sudden Demise
 갑작스러운 최후 is a potent talent on a lot of fights that offers more AoE and add damage. This is especially good on something like Vashnik or Twin Fangs where there are frequent add spawns that need quick swaps to kill them off. In order to take this, the easiest swap to make would be taking the AoE build and dropping the last Apex Talent,  불구대천, and using that point for Sudden Demise instead. This does come with a roughly 2% loss in single-target, so make sure that you are definitely getting value from Sudden Demise when testing it in your raids.

Apex Talent
For higher-level keys where a little more single target/prio damage is wanted, players may opt to take  불구대천 instead of  갑작스러운 최후. Sudden Demise should be more overall damage and more AoE, but comes at the cost of having that up-front single-target damage from the Apex. For push keys, this is the recommended swap, and will serve as the base for the other alternatives below.
No-Zoldyck Builds
Some players have experimented with builds that do not take  졸디크의 비전 at all. Before discussing these swaps, it is important to be clear that these swaps are not recommended builds, and are included in this guide only to explain swaps that have been tested, could be viable, and you might encounter them in your research through data aggregation pages such as Archon or Murlok.

This build has been popularized by some of the early push groups, the argument being that chain-pulled enemies are funnel for the next pack being pulled, where  졸디크의 비전 would be "wasting" damage or "padding". Simply put, this is mathematically incorrect. The damage that is being "lost" in this way is more than offset by the damage gained against the priority targets during the window itself. If targets must die,  졸디크의 비전 works on most every single target ability, including all poisons and  독살. Not taking Zoldyck also hurts Assassination's single target significantly and removes synergy from  갑작스러운 최후, which makes for an incredibly awkward blend of downsides across the board while attempting to capitalize on a very nebulous and cryptic upside.
Avulsion
Another attempted setup drops  갑작스러운 최후 for the apex, and then drops an additional one point of  피의 향기 to get  박리. This can technically be more single-target and is a decent idea, but it usually falls flat, as players tend to heavily underestimate the time spent fighting 2-5 targets. It is very easy to look at a route for a dungeon, find the big pull, and say "look, there's a bunch of targets, 2/2 Scent is bad". It's often very difficult to think about all the smaller enemies that die in those packs, often leaving the group in situations with 3-4 enemies left. In general, it's hard to say that this is worth considering, but it definitely isn't terrible.
Dashing Scoundrel
This setup drops  갑작스러운 최후 for the apex, and then drops an additional one point of  피의 향기 to get  늠름한 불한당. This is similar to the Avulsion setup discussed earlier, but might be a better variant in some situations. While  박리 serves as an all-around pick useful in all situations,  늠름한 불한당 is more targeted at single-target, and because it gives base energy regen, it does not have any scaling into AoE. If you find yourself in low-target situations or a lot of single-target (King's Rest is a good example), Dashing + 1/2 Scent might be a small improvement. Some people also prefer it simply for the extra energy it provides, which can matter in high keys that ask the rogue to press  교란 nearly on cooldown.
BEST ASSASSINATION ROGUE DELVE TALENT BUILD
For Delves, the Mythic+ build works great. We'll take Deathstalker again due to its increased defensive capabilities and frequent resets of  어둡고 어두운 밤. In Delves with solo content, the awkward gameplay of Deathstalker can be mostly ignored, making it much more bearable. If you want to be more chill in your runs,  Fatebound is totally fine as well.
Deathstalker
There is plenty of priority damage and funnel required in delves that can be exploited with  하나 된 집중력, an extra defensive layer from  미끼 상술, and frequent resets for  어둡고 어두운 밤.

If you are having trouble tanking, or your tank Valeera is dying often to melee hits, consider swapping  위축의 독 for  마취 독.  핏빛 약병 can also be a solid choice to take for additional healing.
