 Sunfury is ahead of  Frostfire is most metrics, but there are some advantages.  Frostfire stronger barrier with  주입된 수호물, more execute damage, and built-in slows with  얼음불꽃 화살. It also has a different damage profile with a stronger  유성.  Sunfury is more mobile with  불태우기, and uses more instant cast spells.  Sunfury is the recommended version, but both are viable.
BEST FIRE MAGE RAID TALENT BUILD
The 12.1 tier bonus requires you play  화염 파열. Locking the two points needed for this talent make capstone options very tight, meaning that both versions of Fire now have considerably fewer viable talent options.  Sunfury is much better adapted to  화염 파열 than  Frostfire.

Fire Mage talents remain mostly the same regardless of Hero talent choices or content. The only real swap is  방화광, which is only useful above 90% health. Using  발화 on the pull for better cooldown timings makes the talent less effective. In these situations it can be subbed out, but none of the other options are particularly strong.

BEST FIRE MAGE DELVE TALENT BUILD
 Frostfire is very well suited for Delves, due to the stop-and-go nature of the content. Being able to passively slow mobs with  얼음불꽃 화살 is a huge bonus. Delve builds are very similar to Mythic+
Frostfire
For delves,  Frostfire is recommended for the additional slows.  불타는 격정 and  화상의 자극 are very similar in damage to  완전 연소, which will get less damage when mobs die quicker.
