Right now, Spellslinger is the best for single target, but with the new tier set Frostfire's ability to take advantage of the Season 2 tier set pushes it ahead in many AoE situations. For now, I'm recommending Spellslinger for Delves because the tier set is not as available to solo players early in the season. Due to  등온 핵, Frostfire is locked into  혜성 폭풍, but that's not so bad in Season 2 because talents that synergize with the new tier set like  빙하 분쇄 and  우박 are right next to it.
BEST FROST MAGE RAID TALENT BUILD
In The Venomous Abyss, there are a lot of encounters where you'll be cleaving multiple targets at once. As such, you need to make sure you have  갈라지는 얼음 and  조각난 서리 in your build for most of the raid. The first build listed below deals acceptable single target damage without sacrificing these core cleave talents. For the pure single-target encounters, it's better to skip these with the build in the second tab.

BEST FROST MAGE MYTHIC+ TALENT BUILD
In Season 2, you have the choice of either playing a more standard Spellslinger AoE build like the one played in Season 1, or doing a little more overall damage with Frostfire by skipping your Apex talents. In practice, either option can perform well because Spellslinger has better cooldown flexibility with two charges of  서리 광선 to make up for its lower damage.

BEST FROST MAGE DELVE TALENT BUILD
In Delves, I generally just recommend playing the same build as you are playing in Mythic+. You can choose other utility options in the class tree (for example,  환영 복제 is certainly more valuable in solo content than group content), but generally the gameplay goals are just quite similar to what you'd be doing in Mythic+.
