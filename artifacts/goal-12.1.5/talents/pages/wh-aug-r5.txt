BEST AUGMENTATION EVOKER RAID TALENT BUILD
The recommended raid build for  Chronowarden in Season 2 returns to a more buff-oriented playstyle. While the spec's core rotation remains essentially unchanged, Augmentation's new tier set and stat priority once again encourage choosing  예지 targets and positioning deliberately for  흐르는 모래.

The talent point in  군주 can be swapped into either  미래와 현재 or  엮인 실타래 if it results in more practical cooldown timings for a given encounter.

In a season where  예지 targeting is more relevant, consider taking  신비한 권역 as a solid quality-of-life choice to reduce issues with positioning.  진압 can be dropped if the encounter has no interruptable casts, and  알렉스트라자의 제자 can be dropped on pure single-target encounters. Other swappable talents include  힘의 연결체 (small damage impact) and  시간 수호물 (group healing).

 끓어오르는 비늘 is generally skipped due to its negligible damage output and limited contribution to tank survivability.

BEST AUGMENTATION EVOKER MYTHIC+ TALENT BUILD
The recommended Mythic+ build for  Scalecommander plays  엮인 실타래 instead of base  시간 건너뛰기, and features one major change from the raiding setup.  끓어오르는 비늘 has been recently buffed and now justfies playing  반응성 가죽 in dungeons over other capstone talents. The two capstone points in  임박한 파괴 and  미래 계획 can be swapped out for  신통력 and  군주 for a more Motes-heavy playstyle; these two setups are expected to perform roughly the same in terms of damage output.

This build has few optional, utility talent points, but generally speaking you can swap  힘의 연결체 for utility options like  시간 수호물 or  용족의 조율 at minimal damage cost. Some players might also find value in using  웨이른석 부여 to enable certain skips, or even just to help the healer grab a drink and quickly catch up to the group.

The class tree features more crowd control and utility than a typical raid setup, including  하늘의 공포 and  강대한 휘둘러치기 for additional stops.  몽유병 can be used in place of an interrupt on some caster enemies, and can also enable certain skips for more efficient routing.

 공간의 역설 is recommended as a highly versatile cooldown that can either be used to help your healer in a pinch, or even to help yourself continue to cast when you might otherwise be out of range of enemies. If you don't find yourself using Spatial Paradox often,  시간의 와류 is of course a great alternative, providing you and your party with extra movement options throughout the dungeon.

BEST AUGMENTATION EVOKER DELVE TALENT BUILD
The recommended Delve build for  Scalecommander is now nearly identical to the recommended build for Mythic+, with minimal changes to better support Delve gameplay.

For a remarkably comfortable Delve experience,  끓어오르는 비늘 can be used together with Tank Valeera to deal considerable damage to enemies. While Valeera does not benefit from  칠흑의 힘 and currently cannot be granted  불지옥의 축복, Valeera does benefit from  예지 and  흐르는 모래, and deals particularly impressive damage with  폭격.

Additionally,  시간의 와류 has been swapped in simply to provide more frequent access to  부양.

It should be noted that while Augmentation Evoker can effectively solo Delve content, it is generally recommended to play in a group when playing this specialization; this is where the spec's group utility remains most relevant. Special Delve encounters like the seasonal Delve Nemesis boss continue to be particularly challenging to solo on Augmentation, given that Valeera does not meaningfully contribute to your damage output.
