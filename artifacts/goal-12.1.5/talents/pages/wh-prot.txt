BEST PROTECTION PALADIN MYTHIC+ TALENT BUILD
This build is fairly similar to the raid build, with a couple more AoE oriented talents to ensure we are hitting as many targets as possible and dealing the best damage we can.
Lightsmith
Templar
The playstyle of this build is essentially the same as the raid build. As with most tanks, AoE is baked into our natural toolkit, therefore we always passively cleave. The talents taken just focus on buffing that passive cleave further.
