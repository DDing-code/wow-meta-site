Although both sets of Hero Talents for Brewmaster Monks offer similar amounts of defensive power, each provides it in a different way. The Master of Harmony talents emphasize empowering your defensive abilities such as  정화주 and  천신주/ 천신의 주입, which often make them more suited for progression raiding, while the Shado-Pan talents offer passive damage reduction from consistent dodging like you would experience in an AoE environment such as Mythic+. However, the decision can ultimately come down to personal preference or tuning in a given season; though at present, Shado-Pan's damage quickly gains an advantage at two or more targets while remaining effectively equal in single-target combat.
BEST BREWMASTER MONK RAID TALENT BUILD
Your main focus in determining a single-target raid build will be how much you need to bolster your defenses. In particular, are you comfortable playing with  해악 축출 in a way that rewards quick reactions, or would you benefit more from the steady healing offered by turning  치유의 구슬s into a HoT with  니우짜오의 결의? Are you overwhelmed by the rotation already, or can you handle  흑우주 and altering your rotation during  흑우 니우짜오의 원령? These questions form the core differences between the Standard and Advanced builds below, though other distinctions are noted for each. Make sure to check the "Talent Choices Explained" tab to see the other nodes you may wish to talent into or out of based on a given encounter for a more flexible build in between the two!
Master of Harmony (Standard)
Shado Pan (Advanced)
Raid Talent Choices Explained
This build sacrifices a bit of offensive power for an overall simpler and safer experience, making it an ideal build for players learning to play Brewmaster Monk in a raid setting. It's also a great setup for players in progression raiding environments looking to maximize survivability. In particular, it opts into  니우짜오의 결의,  생기의 불길, and  평온한 정신 for a fair bit of passive defense. If you need even more defense, consider dropping the point of  황소와 함께 걷기 for  강한 내성 or  건우지세.

BEST BREWMASTER MONK MYTHIC+ TALENT BUILD
Mythic+ is often an environment where a combination of AoE and priority-target damage is helpful. Similarly, as there is only one healer present, tanks are expected to be a bit more self-sufficient, though they also frequently take less significant damage than a raid until reaching sufficiently high keystone levels. Due to this, you'll find that a more standard Mythic+ build opts into damage-dealing while an advanced one opts into defense—the opposite of what usually happens in builds made for raiding instead.
Shado Pan (Standard)
Master of Harmony (High Key)
Mythic+ Talent Choices Explained
With the massive amount of different scenarios you'll encounter in a mythic+ dungeon, there is a lot more flexibility to consider. For example, this build defaults to  니우짜오의 결의 and  흑우주 to gain additional bursts of defense alongside a smoother healing experience, but like the advanced raid build above you will eventually be expected to handle regular use of  해악 축출 in higher keystone levels. Likewise,  해독 and  혈점 포착 are not always required in a dungeon, but be warned that not having them when you do need them can be a serious mistake. Further decision making can be found on the "Talent Choices Explained" tab.

BEST BREWMASTER MONK DELVE TALENT BUILD
Delves are frequently run solo by players, which creates an emphasis on self-healing and perhaps even some additional passive defense. Here, the Master of Harmony Hero Talents again have a slight advantage due to the buffs they provide to  범의 장풍 especially. Regardless, in this type of content especially, you can certainly complete it even with the Shado-Pan talents and may even prefer the more passive AoE from  대기 준비.
Master of Harmony (Delves/Open World)
Shado Pan (Legacy/Speed)
Due to Delves and Open World content not expecting you to have a healer present, your incoming damage will be easier to handle and allow you to opt into more offensive talents by default. Although it still selects  니우짜오의 결의 by default, you can certainly play without it here, or even try out  역동의 유동성 for an even faster rotation emphasizing single-target damage.
