Best Midnight Talents for Marksmanship Hunter Marksmanship plays Sentinel in all content, with no exceptions. Dark Ranger is a bit tankier, but Sentinel does more damage and is far easier to play. We provide Dark Ranger builds below for those who prefer the playstyle or thematic fantasy. Single-Target: Sentinel is played in all situations, even though it is slightly less tanky than Dark Ranger . It simply performs better on every metric, and is also easier to play. AoE: Just like in single-target, Sentinel is the go-to choice everywhere. It has the best DPS on every target count, while being easier to play. While it has slightly worse survivability than Dark Ranger , that does not come into play until pushing the absolute top keys, and even then you are typically able to rely on your teammates to help you survive, so you can unload Sentinel's superior DPS. 1.1. Fitting in Tenacious Tenacious and Cunning are our version of having a pet. You pick between 5% extra health with 3% damage reduction, or 8% Movement Speed with Master's Call , which is a freedom effect you can use on yourself or on anyone else. Neither of them do anything for your damage. Our builds run Tenacious by default. In Patch 12.1.5, it is a DPS loss to take Tenacious or Cunning, anywhere from 0.6% to 1.7% depending on the exact scenario. If you are doing anything considered "competitive" content, which in our opinion is anything that you find to be challenging, the loss is worth taking. That said, if you just want to deal the max damage possible, here are the talents you would swap to for each build: Sentinel Single Target - Swap to Deathblow to gain around 0.6% DPS. Sentinel Raid Cleave - Swap to Accuracy By Volume to gain around 1.3% DPS. Sentinel Mythic+ and Delves - Swap to Accuracy By Volume to gain around 1.4% DPS. Dark Ranger Single Target - Swap to Bulletstorm to gain around 1.4% DPS. Dark Ranger Mythic+ and Delves - Swap to Bulletstorm to gain around 1.7% DPS. Sentinel Single-Target Talents This build is our best Sentinel single-target build. It keeps Volley and Explosive Shot , which the Season 2 tier set is built around, and focuses on generating and spending Precise Shots . While raiding, we recommend playing this build any time pure single-target is called for. 1.3. Sentinel Raid AoE/Cleave Talent Build This is the optimal Sentinel build for cleave-heavy fights. It is built around Aspect of the Hydra , grabbing efficient AoE talents like Hydra and Light Ammo to dish out excellent cleave damage while essentially just performing a single-target rotation. Since the raid is so heavy on cleave, this is the build you will use throughout most of it. 1.4. Sentinel Mythic+ Talent Build This is the optimal Sentinel build for Mythic+, and other AoE-focused content. It is based on simulating the conditions found in real Mythic+ dungeons and seeing not just what does the most DPS, but what actually clears the dungeon quicker in a group. There are builds that do more AoE damage, but they all lose more single-target than they gain in terms of AoE, and vice versa. We run Trick Shots in AoE, swapping out Arcane Shot for Multi-Shot to make our abilities do AoE damage. You can also play this build on raid fights that are overwhelmingly single-target but still have occasional bouts of cleave and burst AoE, though that is not a decision to take lightly, as padding AoE is rarely the most effective way to improve your group's chances of success. 1.5. Omnium Folio The Omnium Folio is the "borrowed power" system introduced in Midnight's Patch 12.0.7 , and offers some moderately strong customization options after a short questline. Some of these bonuses affect your damage output, but there are also defensive and mobility options to choose from. Your Folio progress carries over into Season 2. Take these bonuses: Rune of Unleashed Fire Rune of Void-Tainted Shell Rune of Lingering Rune of Critical Power . This one is the most flexible and may change as your gear's stat demands do, just like your Flask or Weapon Enchant. Crit is a safe "never bad" pick, but if you have lots (1300+) of it, you may consider Mastery instead (or sim it). Rune of Overload 3. Apex Talent: Take Aim Midnight introduces new Apex Talents, unlocked between Level 80 and 90. Marksmanship gets: Take Aim Take Aim Take Aim Take Aim increases Rapid Fire damage by 25%, and each shot it fires reduces the cooldown of Aimed Shot by 0.5 seconds, which comes to 5 seconds over a full channel. Take Aim increases the critical damage of your abilities by 5% and the damage of your ranged abilities by 3%, rising to 10% and 6% at its second point. Take Aim makes Spotter's Mark also increase the damage of your next Rapid Fire against the target by 20%. 4. Hero Talents for Marksmanship Hunter Available at level 71, Hero talents are mutually exclusive trees you can choose to slightly alter gameplay and visuals. Marksmanship Hunter can choose between Sentinel and Dark Ranger . 4.1. Dark Ranger Dark Ranger replaces your talented Kill Shot ability with Black Arrow , which deals heavy damage. In addition to being freely usable (with a 10-second cooldown) on targets above 80% or below 20% HP, you will regularly get Deathblow procs that let you use it on any target at any time. Rapid Fire gives a guaranteed proc, and Aimed Shot has a 20% chance to make your next Aimed Shot grant one. When you pop Trueshot , the Trueshot button will turn into Wailing Arrow , a very powerful ability that also gives us a free Black Arrow proc on use. We cast it as soon as we have no Black Arrow proc coming up, usually after a Rapid Fire &rarr; Black Arrow combo. Defensively, Dark Ranger offers Smoke Screen , which is simply amazing, as it gives us extremely easy access to our Survival of the Fittest nearly all the time. 4.2. Sentinel Sentinel is our simplest Hero Talent, adding almost nothing in terms of gameplay intrigue or complexity, replacing Spotter's Mark with Sentinel's Mark , which is simply more powerful in every way. In addition, when you activate Trueshot , the Trueshot button itself will turn into Moonlight Chakram , a filler ability that does heavy damage on use. We only get one use of it, so on single-target we cast it with around 5 seconds left on Trueshot (since its bounces take a long time to finish on single-target), and on AoE right before Trueshot expires (as its bounces are over quickly on AoE). Defensively, Sentinel gives us Don't Look Back , which procs nearly constantly and offers a great deal of passive tankiness. 5. PvP Talents (War Mode) These picks are for open world PvE content with War Mode enabled, not for actual PvP. War Mode offers several benefits for you in the open world. PvP talents enabled in the outdoor world. At least 10% increase in World Quest rewards at maximum level. 10% more experience gained while leveling. Earn Conquest Points, which can reward gear every week. With the benefits of enabling War Mode for leveling and PvE content, it is recommended to enable the feature to maximize your leveling and rewards at maximum level. However, you will be flagged for open-world PvP, and the possibility of being "ganked" while leveling or doing World Quests exists. Aspect of the Fox lets you cast Aimed Shot while moving during Aspect of the Cheetah , and it extends the slower second half of that sprint by 4 seconds. Hunting Pack is a valuable speed increase for leveling and open world content. Sniper's Advantage increases the range of all your shots by 30% during Trueshot and Volley , which can be useful for big elites especially.
