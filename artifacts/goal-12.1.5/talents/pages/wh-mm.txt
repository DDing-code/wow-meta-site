Marksmanship plays  Sentinel in all content, with no exceptions. We provide  Dark Ranger builds above for those who prefer the playstyle or fantasy.
Single Target - Our pure single-target build. It keeps  폭발 사격 and  연발 공격 (with  연사), which the Season 2 tier set is built around, and focuses on generating and spending  정밀 사격.
AoE - We run  교묘한 사격 in AoE, swapping out  신비한 사격 for  일제 사격 to make our abilities do AoE damage. For raid cleave, we like  히드라의 상-based builds. They grab efficient AoE talents like Hydra and  경탄약 to dish out excellent cleave damage while essentially just performing a single-target rotation.
For Raiding, since the raid is so heavy on cleave fights, the cleave build is the most common throughout the raid. Any time pure single-target is called for, swap to the single-target build. On fights that are overwhelmingly single-target, but still have occasional bouts of cleave and burst AoE, we can play our Mythic+ build, but that is not a decision to be taken lightly as padding AoE is rarely the most effective way to improve your group's chances of success.
TRADING SURVIVABILITY FOR DPS
 불굴 and  교활 are the MM version of having a pet. You get to choose between +5% HP + 3% damage reduction, or 8% Movement Speed +  주인의 부름, which is a freedom effect for you or anyone else. Neither does any damage.

Every build above takes  불굴. 12.1 moved the node off the path to  정조준, so it costs DPS now where it used to be free. Survivability is always useful and for competitive content we'd give up about 1-2% damage for it. If you would rather have the damage, here is what talent swaps you would make for each build.
 Sentinel Single Target - take  죽음의 강타, about 0.6% damage.
 Sentinel Raid AoE/Cleave - take  물량 공세, about 1.3%.
 Sentinel Mythic+ and Delves - take  물량 공세, about 1.4%.
 Dark Ranger Single Target - take  총알 세례, about 1.4%.
 Dark Ranger Mythic+ and Delves - take  총알 세례, about 1.7%.
