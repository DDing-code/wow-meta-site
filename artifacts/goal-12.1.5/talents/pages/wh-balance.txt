Heading into Midnight Season 2 you are likely going to be seeing an increase in builds playing  Elune's Chosen compared to previously. It is currently the highest performing hero talent tree, and started gaining popularity after the halfway point of Season 1, both on Single Target and AoE scenarios. That doesn't mean that there aren't moments where  Keeper of the Grove would be a better choice, like on bosses with a lot of spread cleave and adds that spawn on static timers, like the ones we saw a lot of in The Voidspire during Season 1. There are a couple of those fights this tier as well, and you could very well see  Keeper of the Grove played on those.

The biggest difference you will notice with the Single-Target builds for  Elune's Chosen from previously is that you opt for  비전 친화 over  삭망월, which means you get considerably less casts of  엘룬의 분노 and have to plan your  화신: 엘룬의 선택 and  월식 damage windows around it.

For mythic+, the general recommended choice is still  Elune's Chosen. You could make a case for  Keeper of the Grove on dungeons with smaller or more spread out pulls.  Elune's Chosen excels at stacked cleave, while  Keeper of the Grove leans into overall buffing your damage and reacting to procs.
