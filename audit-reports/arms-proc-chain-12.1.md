# Arms charge and reset chain

Verified 2026-09-22 with Korean Wowhead tooltip IDs 184783, 1261057, 386630, 386634 and the 12.1.0.69875 SimulationCraft trait snapshot.

- Tactician: 30% per eligible Rage-cost attack, including attacks whose cost is reduced to zero; not a probability per Rage point.
- Master Tactician: tooltip substitution changes 30 to 35, establishing five percentage points rather than a 1.05 multiplier.
- Battlelord: Overpower has a 35% chance to reset Mortal Strike and reduce its next cost by 33%; distinguish from Tactician's Overpower charge.
- Executioner's Precision: Execute strengthens the next Mortal Strike by 35%, maximum two stacks. Not an Execute damage buff.

Added the missing Master Tactician KB spell and a targeted charge-reset synergy. Updated three existing notes and guide sections 4 and 6. Internal tooltip cooldowns were not presented as active-button cooldowns.
