# Colossus Smash follow-up talents

Verified 2026-09-22. Added canonical spells 1261056 Crushing Combo (궤멸적인 연계) and 1261048 Just Warming Up (몸풀기였을 뿐), their resource/cooldown synergy, and guide explanations.

Current Crushing Combo tooltip: next two Cleaves within 20 seconds of Colossus Smash trigger no cooldown. Do not reuse the older three-cast value. This does not waive Rage or the global cooldown, nor extend Colossus Smash's damage debuff to 20 seconds.

Just Warming Up grants 15 Rage through Colossus Smash. Do not grant this amount to builds without the talent. Two no-cooldown Cleaves are not fully funded by 15 Rage alone.
